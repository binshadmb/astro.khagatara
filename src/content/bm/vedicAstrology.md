# Free Védic astrology: jyotish ka yeelen Intɛrinɛti kan

jyotish—Védic astrology—a bɛ fɔ a ma laada la ko “yeelen dɔnniya.” Ni tariku ma tiɲɛ, a ka ca ni san 5000 ye, a ye yɛrɛfaamucogo camanba dɔ ye, hadamadenw ye min labɛn. Sanni a ka mɔgɔya jogo bɛɛ lajɛlen ɲɛfɔ dɔrɔn, jyotish bɛ diɲɛ cogoya juguw ni waati sɛrɛkiliw sɛgɛsɛgɛ minnu bɛ dɛmɛ don ka bɛn don i ka ɲɛnamaya kɔnɔ.

---

## jyotish ye mun ye?

jyotish ye Sanskrikan daɲɛ ye min kɔrɔ ye "yeelen dɔnniya" walima "sankololafɛnw dɔnniya." O ye Vedanga wɔɔrɔ dɔ ye—Vɛdaw ka kalan dɛmɛnanw, sɛbɛn senuma kɔrɔw. jyotish dabɔra Rishiw fɛ — Ɛndu kɔrɔlen filɛlikɛla hakilitigiw — minnu ye diɲɛ jatebɔcogo dafalenw kɔlɔsi ani u y’a faamu ko u ni hadamaden hakili bɛ ɲɔgɔn na.

---

## jyotish ka bolofara saba

jyotish ka dɔnniyaba tilalen don jɔyɔrɔba saba ye:

1. 1. .**Ganita (Sɔrɔko ni jatebɔ):**Jateblaw jusigilan. A bɛ dolow ni planetew jɔyɔrɔ tigitigi jate ni sidereal zodiac ye—dolow kuluw jɔyɔrɔ yɛrɛyɛrɛ minnu bɛ sankolo la bi.
2. 2. Ɲɛgɛnɛsiraw ani mɔgɔ dogoyɔrɔw.**Hora (Bange ka kalan):**Bangesɛbɛn kelen-kelen bɛɛ sɛgɛsɛgɛli. A bɛ sɛgɛsɛgɛli kɛ cogo min na dugukolow bɛnkanw bɛ bɛn mɔgɔ kelen-kelen bɛɛ jogo ma, a fanga ani a ka ɲɛnamaya sira ma.
3. 3. .**Samhita (Kalan min bɛ kɛ diɲɛ kɔnɔ):**Jɛkulu ka ko kɛlenw, sigida cogoyaw, ani hadamadenw ka siwiliw ka sɛrɛkilibaw kalanni.

## nakshatra 27: Kalo sobaw

nakshatras ye Védic astrology ka kalo so 27 ye — Kalo bɛ taama kalo o kalo-yɔrɔ la. I bange nakshatra ye i ka jatebɔsɛbɛn kɔnɔfɛn nafama dɔ ye, min bɛ i hakili ka cogoya dafalen jira:

*   **Ashwini:**Teliya, kɛnɛya fanga, ani daminɛ kura.
*   **Bharani ye:**Yɛlɛma jugumanba ni kurayali.
*   **Krittika: 1.**Sinsin, ɲɛfɔli jɛlen ani kololi saniya.
*   **Rohini ye:**Daɲɛw dacogo, cɛɲi ani dusukunnataw ta fan fɛ.
*   **Mrigashira ye:**Ɲininkali, ɲinini, ani ɲinini min bɛ kɛ ni hakili ye.
*   **Ardra:**Sanfiɲɛw saniya ani daɲɛw kurayali.
*   **Punarvasu ye:**Seginkanni, lakanani, ani daminɛ kura.
*   **Pushya:**Alako ta fan fɛ balo, hakilitigiya ani ladonni.
*   **Ashlesha ye:**Gundo, dunuya, ani kɔnɔna fanga min sinsinnen don.
*   *(Ani nakshatra cɛɲi 18 wɛrɛw, u kelen-kelen bɛɛ bɛ u yɛrɛ ka yɛrɛyɛrɛ fanga kɛrɛnkɛrɛnnen ta)*

## vimshottari dasha: Planɛtiw ka sɛrɛkiliw

vimshottari dasha ye planete waatiw sigicogo ye min dalen bɛ a la kosɛbɛ jyotish kɔnɔ. A bɛ hadamaden ka ɲɛnamaya kɛcogo dafalen min bɛ se ka kɛ san 120 ye, o jate i n’a fɔ dugukolo waatiw tugu-tugu ɲɔgɔn na, minnu kelen-kelen bɛɛ bɛ mara ni dugukolo fanga kɔnɔntɔn dɔ ye:

*   **Ketu (San 7):**Alako ta fan fɛ hakilijakabɔ, kɔnɔna miirili, ani jirisun tɛmɛnenw.
*   **Venus (San 20):**Daɲɛw bɛnkan, jɛɲɔgɔnyaw, ani fɛnw nɔgɔyali.
*   **Tile (San 6):**Kɔnɔna fanga, sinsin, kuntilenna ani jɛya.
*   **Kalo (San 10):**Dusukunnataw ladonni, sɔnni, ani denbaya jujɔ.
*   **Marisikalo (San 7):**Fanga nafama, sinsin, jagɛlɛya ani wale.
*   **Rahu (San 18):**Hakilitigiya, jamana wɛrɛw ka ɲɛtaa, ani diɲɛ kalanw.
*   **Jupiter (San 16):**Hakilitigiya, bonya alako ta fan fɛ, dɔ farali, ani kalan.
*   **Saturne (San 19):**Kololi, kunkanbaaraw, muɲuli ani kalan sabatilen.
*   **Mɛkisiki (San 17): .**Hakilila jɛlen, jago, ani kumaɲɔgɔnya.

## Un Espejo de tu Alma: A ka baara kɛcogo ɲuman

Nin tɛ kunnafoni ye min bɛ kɛ k’a sɔrɔ a ma kɛ cogo si la, min bɛ dilan ni porogaramuw ye minnu bɛ wele ko generic software. O ye diɲɛ karti ye min ɲɛfɔlen don kosɛbɛ, min ye tiɲɛ ye, min jatera ni jatebɔ tigitigi ye ni kalo tɛmɛsira lakikaw ye sankololafɛnw na ani Védic ni Kaldeanw ka formulaw bɛnbaw fɛ. An ka laɲini kelenpe ye ka jɛya, bonya, ani baarakɛminɛn lakika di aw ma aw yɛrɛ sɔrɔli ni hakilijagabɔ kama.

### Mun bɛ makɔnɔni na i yɛrɛ ka ɲɛ 8 jatebɔsɛbɛn (PDF) kɔnɔ?

*   **Aw ka Siginiden Yɛrɛyɛrɛli:**Sɛgɛsɛgɛli jugu kɛ i ka Ɲɛnamaya sira nimɔrɔ kan, i ka Tɔgɔ nimɔrɔ (Kaldekaw ka sigida), ani i ka Ni nege weleli makuntɔ.
*   **Furu ɲɔgɔndan & Jɛɲɔgɔnya Bɛn:**Aw ka jɛɲɔgɔnya cogoyaw faamuyali, aw ka bange jatebɔsɛbɛn bɛ fanga kɛrɛnkɛrɛnnenw sama cogo min na, ani jatebɔ laadalata minnu bɛ kɛ walasa ka furu jɛɲɔgɔnya juguw ni banbaliw sigi sen kan, minnu sinsinnen bɛ bɛnkan kan.
*   **Aw ka dɛmɛ fanga:**Laadalata hakilinaw kulɛriw kan, kabakurun sɔngɔ gɛlɛnw mankan, ani tile minnu bɛ ɲɔgɔn bɛn, minnu bɛ dusu don i kɔnɔ i ka i sinsin i ka don o don ɲɛnamaya kɔnɔ, i ka bɛn i ma, ani i ka ko ɲumanw kɛ.
*   **I ka waati taabolo faamuyali (vimshottari dasha):**I bɛ ka planete laadala waati min sɔrɔ sisan, o sɛgɛsɛgɛli, o bɛ kɛ i n’a fɔ sigida kɔrɔlen ye i yɛrɛ ka miiri, muɲu ani kɔnɔna waati la.
*   **Kɔlɔsili min bɛ kɛ ni hakilijakabɔ ye:**Nin kunnafoni in ye jatebɔ kɛcogo kalan ye, min dabɔra walasa ka yɛrɛlajɛ ni mɔgɔ yɛrɛ ka bonya dɛmɛ.
