# Bangesɛbɛn fu: Aw ka Védic kundali Online

I wolosɛbɛn ye sankolow karti ye i wolo waati tigitigi la. O ye diɲɛ ja ye i sera nin diɲɛ in na waati tigitigi min na. Védic astrology, n’a ka tariku ka ca ni san 5000 ye, o bɛ baara kɛ ni nin ja in ye walasa k’i kɔnɔna seko jira, i ka dacogo negew, ani diɲɛ kɔnɔko minnu bɛ i fanga dilan.

---

## Védic Bangesɛbɛn ye mun ye?

A tɛ i n’a fɔ Tlebi Dolow dɔnniya min bɛ a jigi da Tle taamaʃyɛn kan kosɛbɛ, Védic Dolow dɔnniya bɛ sinsin Kalo taamaʃyɛn kan—taamaʃyɛn min ye Kalo tùn bɛ yɔrɔ min na i wolotuma na. Nin Kalo Taamaʃyɛn in, min bɛ Weele ko**rashi ye**sanskrikan na, o bɛ i dusukunnataw cogoya jugumanba jira, i ka hakilinaw, ani i hakili kɔnɔna na.

---

## Védic kalo taamasiyɛn 12 (rashi) .

Védic zodiac tilalen don taamasiyɛn 12 ye, u kelen-kelen bɛɛ bɛ fanga kɛrɛnkɛrɛnnen dɔ ta:

*   **Mesha (Aries) ye:**Tutigɛbaga, jagɛlɛya ani dusu ka di.
*   **Vrishabha (Taurus) ye:**A sabatilen don, a bɛ muɲu ani a bɛ muɲuli kosɛbɛ.
*   **Mithuna (Gemini) ye:**A bɛ ɲininkali kɛ, a bɛ kumaɲɔgɔnya kɛ, wa a bɛ se ka fɛn caman kɛ.
*   **Karka (Kansɛri): .**A bɛ faamuyali sɔrɔ, a bɛ mɔgɔ tanga, wa a bɛ dusukunnataw ta kosɛbɛ.
*   **Simha (Leo) ye:**A bɛ daɲɛw da, a bɛ bolomafara di, ani a bɛ fanga sɔrɔ a dacogo la.
*   **Kanya (Npogotigi):**Sɛgɛsɛgɛli, a yɛrɛdi, ani dɛmɛba.
*   **Tula (Libra) ye:**Bɛn, tilennenya, ani diplomasi dacogo la.
*   **Vrishchika (Escorpio) ye:**A fanga ka bon, a bɛ fɛn caman sɛmɛntiya, wa a sinsinnen don kosɛbɛ.
*   **Dhanu (Sagittaire) ye:**Filɛsofi, ɲɛnajɛla ani jigiyatigi.
*   **Makara (Kapirikɔni):**A kololilen don, a bɛ ŋaniyaba ta, ani a bɛ baara kɛ kosɛbɛ.
*   **Kumbha (Aquarius) ye:**A bɛ fɛn kuraw don, a bɛ hadamadenya dɛmɛ, wa a bɛ daminɛ kosɛbɛ.
*   **Meena (Jɛgɛw):**A bɛ hinɛ mɔgɔw la, a bɛ mɔgɔw faamuya kosɛbɛ, ani a bɛ alako ta fan fɛ.

## nakshatra walima Bange Dolow ye mun ye?

nakshatra ye i wolo dolow ye—kalo soba, Kalo tun sigilen bɛ yɔrɔ min na i wolotuma na. Védic astrology bɛ zodiac tila ka kɛ nakshatra 27 ye, minnu kelen-kelen bɛɛ bɛ mara fanga kɛrɛnkɛrɛnnen dɔ fɛ, ala min bɛ fanga la ani kuntilenna kɛrɛnkɛrɛnnen dɔ fɛ. I ka nakshatra b’i ka jogo juguba jira, hakili ta fan fɛ nilifɛnw, ani fanga kɛrɛnkɛrɛnnen minnu nana i dɔn nin ɲɛnamaya kɔnɔ.

## dasha Sistɛmu: Planɛtiw ka waati sɛrɛkiliw

dasha sitɛmu ye Védic astrology ka baarakɛminɛn juguba dɔ ye. A b’i ka ɲɛnamaya tila waatibaw la minnu bɛ mara dugukolo suguya wɛrɛw fɛ, u kelen-kelen bɛɛ bɛ na ni fanga nɔ kɛrɛnkɛrɛnnen ye. I ka sisan dasha faamuyali b’a to i bɛ se ka dugukolo fanga min bɛ fanga la, o faamuya min bɛ nɔ bila i hakili la sisan, ka mɔgɔ yɛrɛ ka dɔnniya ni hakilijagabɔ dɛmɛ.

*   **Furu ɲɔgɔndan & Jɛɲɔgɔnya Bɛn:**Aw ka jɛɲɔgɔnya cogoyaw faamuyali, aw ka bange jatebɔsɛbɛn bɛ fanga kɛrɛnkɛrɛnnenw sama cogo min na, ani jatebɔ laadalata minnu bɛ kɛ walasa ka furu jɛɲɔgɔnya juguw ni banbaliw sigi sen kan, minnu sinsinnen bɛ bɛnkan kan.

*   **Kɔlɔsili min bɛ kɛ ni hakilijakabɔ ye:**Nin kunnafoni in ye jatebɔ kɛcogo kalan ye, min dabɔra walasa ka yɛrɛlajɛ ni mɔgɔ yɛrɛ ka bonya dɛmɛ.
