## The Three Core Name Numbers

Your name chart is composed of three vital numbers:

1. **Expression Number (Name Number):** Calculated using all the letters of your full birth name. It reveals your natural talents, strengths, and how you express your life purpose to the world.
2. **Soul Urge Number (Heart's Desire):** Calculated using only the vowels of your name. It reveals your deepest inner motivations, secret yearnings, and what truly satisfies your soul from within.
3. **Personality Number:** Calculated using only the consonants of your name. It reveals how others perceive you, the immediate impression you make, and the outer persona you present.
