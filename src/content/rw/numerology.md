# Gusoma Numero Yubusa: Menya Igishushanyo mbonera cyawe

Numerology ni imwe muri sisitemu ya kera yo kwishakamo ibisubizo. Mu myaka ibihumbi, imico kwisi yose yakoresheje imibare kugirango yumve imiterere, umubano, nubuzima busanzwe bwubuzima. Uyu munsi, urashobora kubara inzira yawe yubuzima hamwe numubare wisi wubusa rwose hamwe na sisitemu ya Chaldean na Védic numerology.

---

## Umubare w'inzira y'ubuzima ni iki?

Umubare Winzira Yubuzima Numubare wingenzi mubishushanyo mbonera byawe. Bikomoka kumibare nyayo y'amavuko yawe. Uyu mubare ukora nk'indorerwamo yo mu mwuka, ugaragaza imico yawe yibanze, impano zawe, n'amasomo yihariye yo mu mwuka wahisemo gucukumbura muri ubu buzima.

---

## Nigute Wabara Ubuzima bwawe Inzira

Kubara Inzira Yinzira Yubuzima, urateranya imibare yose yumunsi wamavuko (umunsi, ukwezi, numwaka) hanyuma ukabigabanya kumubare umwe hagati ya 1 na 9.

Ariko, niba amafaranga yawe angana**11, 22, cyangwa 33**, ntugabanye. Ibi byitwa**Umubare Wibanze**kandi uhagararire kunyeganyega hejuru cyane no kwiyerekana cyane.

### Intambwe ku yindi
Niba waravutse ku ya 13 Kamena 1981:
1.**Mugabanye umunsi:**13 → 1 + 3 = 4
2.**Mugabanye ukwezi:**Kamena ni ukwezi kwa 6 → 6
3.**Mugabanye umwaka:**1981 → 1 + 9 + 8 + 1 = 19 → 1 + 9 = 10 → 1 + 0 = 1
4.**Incamake Yuzuye:**4 (Umunsi) + 6 (Ukwezi) + 1 (Umwaka) = 11
* Igisubizo:**Inzira y'ubuzima 11**- Umwigisha Intuitive. *

*   **3 (Ihanga):**Kugaragaza, gushyikirana, ubuhanzi, kandi birashimishije.
*   **4 (Umwubatsi):**Indero, ifatika, ihamye, kandi yizewe cyane.
*   **5 (Abadiventiste):**Ubuntu-bwisanzure, butandukanye, amatsiko, nimbaraga.
*   **6 (Umurezi):**Ushinzwe, kwita, kurinda, no kwita kumuryango.

*   **9 (Ubumuntu):**Impuhwe, ubuntu, ubwitange, nubwenge rusange.

## Védic na Western Numerology

Mugihe Western Numerology (Pythagorean) ikunzwe cyane,**Imibare ya Védic**(bikomoka mu Buhinde bwa kera) bihuza imibare itaziguye n’ukwezi kw’ikirere.

Aho kureba imibare mu bwigunge, uburyo bwa Védic buhuza itariki yawe y'amavuko n'ikimenyetso cyawe cy'ukwezi (**Rashi**), Inyenyeri Yavutse (**Nakshatra**), hamwe n'umubumbe w'iki gihe (**Dasha**). Ibi bitanga uburyo bwuzuye bwuzuye, bwimbitse, kandi bwuzuzanya muburyo bwo guhuza imbaraga.
