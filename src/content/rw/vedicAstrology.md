# Ubuntu bwa Védic Astrology: Umucyo wa Jyotish Kumurongo

Jyotish - Védic Astrology - isanzwe izwi nka "siyanse y'umucyo." Hamwe nimyaka irenga 5.000 yamateka atavunitse, ni bumwe muburyo bukomeye bwo kwiyumvisha ibintu bwatejwe imbere nubumuntu. Aho gusobanura gusa imico rusange, Jyotish akora ubushakashatsi bwimbitse bwikirere hamwe nigihe cyigihe kigufasha kuzana ubwuzuzanye mubuzima bwawe.

---

## Jyotish ni iki?

Jyotish ni ijambo rya Sanskrit risobanura "siyanse yumucyo" cyangwa "siyanse yimibiri yo mwijuru." Ni imwe muri esheshatu za Vedangasi - inyigisho zifasha za Veda, inyandiko zera za kera. Jyotish yatejwe imbere na Rishis - abanyabwenge babonaga Ubuhinde bwa kera - bakurikiranaga imibare itunganijwe neza y'isi kandi bakamenya isano ifitanye n'ubwenge bwa muntu.

---

## Amashami atatu ya Jyotish

Ubumenyi bunini bwa Jyotish bugabanijwemo inkingi eshatu zingenzi:

1.**Ganita (Astronomie na Kubara):**Urufatiro rw'imibare. Irabara imyanya nyayo yinyenyeri numubumbe ukoresheje zodiac kuruhande - imyanya ifatika yinyenyeri zo mwijuru muri iki gihe.
2.**Hora (Kwiga Natal):**Isesengura ry'imbonerahamwe y'amavuko. Yiga uburyo guhuza umubumbe ukivuka bihuye nimiterere yumuntu, imbaraga, n'inzira y'ubuzima.
3.**Samhita (Kwiga Mundane):**Kwiga ibyabaye hamwe, imiterere karemano, hamwe ninzinguzingo nini yimico yabantu.

## 27 Nakshatras: Inzu y'ukwezi

Nakshatras ni inzu 27 y’ukwezi kwa Védic Astrology-inyenyeri inyuramo ukwezi kuzenguruka mu kwezi kwayo. Ivuka ryawe Nakshatra ni kimwe mu bintu by'ingenzi bigize imbonerahamwe yawe, byerekana imiterere y'ubwenge bwawe:

*   **Ashwini:**Umuvuduko, imbaraga zo gukiza, n'intangiriro nshya.
*   **Bharani:**Guhinduka cyane no kuvugurura.
*   **Krittika:**Kweza intumbero, gusobanuka, na disipulini.
*   **Rohini:**Guhanga, ubwiza, nubushyuhe bwamarangamutima.
*   **Mrigashira:**Amatsiko, gushakisha, nubushakashatsi bworoshye.
*   **Ardra:**Kwoza umuyaga no kuvugurura guhanga.
*   **Punarvasu:**Kugarura, kurinda, nintangiriro nshya.
*   **Pushya:**Intungamubiri zo mu mwuka, ubwenge, no kwitaho.
*   **Ashlesha:**Amayobera, ubujyakuzimu, n'imbaraga zimbere.
*   * (Kandi 18 nziza cyane Nakshatras, buriwese yitwaje imbaraga zidasanzwe zo kunyeganyega) *

## Vimshottari Dasha: Umuzenguruko

Vimshottari Dasha nuburyo bwizewe cyane bwibihe byimibumbe muri Jyotish. Ireba ubuzima bwuzuye bwubuzima bwumuntu bwimyaka 120 nkurukurikirane rwibihe byimibumbe, buri kimwe kiyobowe nimwe mumbaraga icyenda zumubumbe:

*   **Ketu (Imyaka 7):**Gutekereza mu mwuka, gutekereza imbere, n'imizi yashize.
*   **Venus (Imyaka 20):**Guhuza guhanga, umubano, no kunonosorwa.
*   **Izuba (Imyaka 6):**Imbaraga zimbere, kwibanda, intego, no gusobanuka.
*   **Ukwezi (Imyaka 10):**Kurera amarangamutima, kwakirwa, n'imizi y'umuryango.
*   **Mars (Imyaka 7):**Imbaraga zingirakamaro, kwibanda, ubutwari, nigikorwa.
*   **Rahu (Imyaka 18):**Kwifuza, gutambuka mumahanga, namasomo yisi.
*   **Jupiter (Imyaka 16):**Ubwenge, gukura mu mwuka, kwaguka, no kwiga.
*   **Saturne (Imyaka 19):**Indero, inshingano, kwihangana, no kwiga gukomeye.
*   **Mercure (Imyaka 17):**Ubwenge busobanutse, ubucuruzi, n'itumanaho.
