# Chart Haihuwa Kyauta: Védic Kundali Kan layi

Taswirar haihuwarku ita ce taswirar sammai a daidai lokacin da aka haife ku. Hoton sararin samaniya ne a daidai lokacin da kuka isa wannan duniyar. Védic Astrology, tare da fiye da shekaru 5,000 na tarihi, yana amfani da wannan ginshiƙi don bayyana yuwuwar ku na ciki, sha'awar halitta, da kuma zagayowar sararin samaniya waɗanda ke siffanta kuzarinku.

---

## Menene Chart Haihuwa Védic?

Ba kamar Western Astrology wanda ke dogaro da Alamar Rana ba, Védic Astrology yana mai da hankali kan Alamar Wata—alamar da aka sanya wata a lokacin haifuwar ku. Wannan Alamar Wata, da ake kira**Rashi**a cikin Sanskrit, yana wakiltar zurfafan yanayin motsin zuciyar ku, illolin ku, da kuma tunanin ku na hankali.

---

Zodiac Védic ya kasu kashi 12 alamu, kowanne yana ɗauke da makamashi na musamman:

*   **Mesha (Aries):**Majagaba, jajircewa, da kishi.
*   **Vrishabha (Taurus):**Barga, haƙuri, da juriya sosai.
*   **Mithuna (Gemini):**Mai ban sha'awa, sadarwa, kuma mai yawan iyawa.
*   **Karka (Cancer):**Hankali, mai karewa, da zurfafa tunani.
*   **Simha (Leo):**Ƙirƙira, mai karimci, kuma mai iko ta halitta.
*   **Kanya (Virgo):**Nazari, sadaukarwa, da taimako sosai.
*   **Tula (Libra):**Mai jituwa, adalci, kuma diflomasiyya ta halitta.
*   **Vrishchika (Escorpio):**M, mai canzawa, da mai da hankali sosai.
*   **Dhanu (Sagittarius):**Falsafa, jajircewa, da kyakkyawan fata.

## Menene Nakshatra ko Tauraron Haihuwa?

Nakshatra ita ce tauraruwar haifuwar ku - gidan wata inda wata ya zauna a lokacin haifuwar ku. Védic Astrology ya raba zodiac zuwa Nakshatras 27, kowannensu yana sarrafa ta takamaiman makamashi, abin bautawa, da manufa. Nakshatra naku yana bayyana zurfin halin ku, kyaututtukan ruhi, da takamaiman ƙarfin da kuka zo don ƙware a wannan rayuwar.

---

## Tsarin Dasha: Tsarin Lokacin Duniya

Tsarin Dasha ɗaya ne daga cikin manyan kayan aikin Védic Astrology. Yana raba rayuwar ku zuwa manyan lokuta da taurari daban-daban ke mulki, kowanne yana kawo tasiri na musamman mai kuzari. Fahimtar Dasha ɗin ku na yanzu yana ba ku damar fahimtar ƙarfin duniyar duniyar da ke mamaye hankalin ku a yanzu, yana tallafawa wayewar kai da tunani.

## Un Espejo de tu Alma: Keɓaɓɓen Tsarin Tsarin Duniya

Wannan ba rahoton bazuwar software ne da aka samar. Cikakkiyar taswirar sararin samaniya ce daki-daki, wanda aka lissafta tare da madaidaicin lissafi ta amfani da hanyoyin wucewar wata na zahiri da tsarin kakannin Védic da Kaldiya. Manufar mu kawai ita ce samar muku da tsabta, girmamawa, da kayan aiki na gaske don gano kanku da tunani.

### Menene ke jira a cikin keɓaɓɓen zane mai shafi 8 (PDF)?

*   **Sa hannu na Jijjiga ku:**Bincike mai zurfi na Lambar Hanyar Rayuwarku, Lambar Sunanku (tsarin Kaldiya), da kiran shiru na Ƙarfafa Rawar ku.
*   **Daidaita Aure & Jituwar Dangantaka:**Fahimtar tsarin dangantakar ku, yadda jadawalin haihuwarku ke jawo takamaiman kuzari, da maɓallan lissafi na al'ada don gina zurfafa, dorewar ɗaurin aure bisa dacewa.
*   **Ƙarfafan Ƙarfin ku:**Fahimtar al'ada game da launuka, jin daɗin gemstone, da kwanakin daidaitawa waɗanda ke ƙarfafa mayar da hankali, jituwa, da haɓakawa a cikin rayuwar yau da kullun.
*   **Fahimtar Zagayowar Lokacinku na Yanzu (Vimshottari Dasha):**Binciko zamanin duniyar duniyar da kuke fuskanta a halin yanzu, yin aiki azaman tsohuwar tsarin tunani, haƙuri, da lokacin ciki.
*   **Bayanin Tunani:**Wannan rahoto nazari ne na tsarin lissafi, wanda aka tsara don tallafawa tunanin kai da ci gaban mutum.
