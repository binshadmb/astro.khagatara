# Jadawalin Astrology Kyauta: Gano Daidaitawar Gefenku

Taswirar taurarinku ya fi jerin jerin taurari da alamu masu sauƙi nesa ba kusa ba. Harshen lissafin sama ne da aka rubuta a daidai lokacin da aka haife ku. Védic Astrology (Jyotish), wanda ya samo asali a tsohuwar Indiya, yana ba da ɗaya daga cikin madaidaicin fassarorin sararin samaniya da zurfin ruhi na wannan taswirar sararin samaniya.

---

## Menene Taswirar Taurari na Védic?

Chart na Taurari na Védic, wanda aka fi sani da a**Kundali**ko**jadawalin haihuwa**, cikakken taswirar geometric ne na matsayi na duniya a daidai lokacin haihuwar ku. Ba kamar horoscopes na Yammacin Turai ba, Jyotish yana amfani da**zodiac na gefe**—Ainihin matsayi na zahiri na taurari da taurari a sararin sama a yau—wanda ya sa ya zama daidai sosai.

---

## Taurari na Védic guda 9 (Grahas)

A cikin Jyotish, jikunan taurari tara suna wakiltar ainihin abubuwan da ke cikin sani da kuzarinmu:

*   **Surya (Sun):**Rai, jagoranci, manufa, da ƙarfin ciki.
*   **Chandra (wata):**Hankali, motsin rai, renon uwa, da hankali.
*   **Mangala (Mars):**Mutunci, tuƙi, ƙarfin hali, aiki, da kuzarin jiki.
*   **Budha (Mercury):**Hankali, sadarwa, tsabtar nazari, da kasuwanci.
*   **Guru (Jupiter):**Hikima, haɓakar ruhaniya, faɗaɗawa, nazari, da alheri.
*   **Shukra (Venus):**Ƙauna, jituwa na fasaha, dangantaka, gyare-gyare, da kyau.
*   **Shani (Saturn):**Ladabi, haƙuri, nauyi, da tsari.
*   **Rahu (Node ta Arewa):**Sha'awar magana, darussan abin duniya, ƙirƙira, da faɗaɗawa.
*   **Ketu (Node ta Kudu):**Sakin ciki, rabewar ruhaniya, tushen baya, da 'yanci.

---

## Sidereal vs. Tsarin Zodiac na wurare masu zafi

Western Astrology yana amfani da**zodiac na wurare masu zafi**, wanda aka kayyade zuwa yanayi na shekara. Védic Astrology yana amfani da**zodiac na gefe**, wanda aka makala zuwa ainihin matsayin taurari.

Saboda ƙayyadaddun ma'auni, a halin yanzu akwai bambanci kusan digiri 23 tsakanin tsarin biyu. Wannan shine dalilin da yasa Alamar Watan Védic ko Hawan hawan ku sau da yawa ya bambanta da alamar ku ta Yamma-daidaita kai tsaye tare da gaskiyar zahirin sararin samaniya.

---

## Fahimtar Zagayowar Lokaci Mai kuzarinku

Ɗaya daga cikin mafi mahimmancin al'amuran ginshiƙi na Védic shine ikonsa na taswirar zagayowar makamashi akan lokaci. Yin amfani da Vimshottari Dasha da zirga-zirgar sararin samaniya, za mu iya fahimtar lokutan duniyar duniyar da ke rinjayar tunanin ku, kiwon lafiya, da filayen dangantaka, samar da kyakkyawan madubi don sanin kai da kuma rayuwa mai hankali.

## Un Espejo de tu Alma: Keɓaɓɓen Tsarin Tsarin Duniya

Wannan ba rahoton bazuwar software ne da aka samar. Cikakkiyar taswirar sararin samaniya ce daki-daki, wanda aka lissafta tare da madaidaicin lissafi ta amfani da hanyoyin wucewar wata na zahiri da tsarin kakannin Védic da Kaldiya. Manufar mu kawai ita ce samar muku da tsabta, girmamawa, da kayan aiki na gaske don gano kanku da tunani.

### Menene ke jira a cikin keɓaɓɓen zane mai shafi 8 (PDF)?

*   **Sa hannu na Jijjiga ku:**Bincike mai zurfi na Lambar Hanyar Rayuwarku, Lambar Sunanku (tsarin Kaldiya), da kiran shiru na Ƙarfafa Rawar ku.
*   **Daidaita Aure & Jituwar Dangantaka:**Fahimtar tsarin dangantakar ku, yadda jadawalin haihuwarku ke jawo takamaiman kuzari, da maɓallan lissafi na al'ada don gina zurfafa, dorewar ɗaurin aure bisa dacewa.
*   **Ƙarfafan Ƙarfin ku:**Fahimtar al'ada game da launuka, jin daɗin gemstone, da kwanakin daidaitawa waɗanda ke ƙarfafa mayar da hankali, jituwa, da haɓakawa a cikin rayuwar yau da kullun.
*   **Fahimtar Zagayowar Lokacinku na Yanzu (Vimshottari Dasha):**Binciko zamanin duniyar duniyar da kuke fuskanta a halin yanzu, yin aiki azaman tsohuwar tsarin tunani, haƙuri, da lokacin ciki.
*   **Bayanin Tunani:**Wannan rahoto nazari ne na tsarin lissafi, wanda aka tsara don tallafawa tunanin kai da ci gaban mutum.
