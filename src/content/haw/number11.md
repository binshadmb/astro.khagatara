# Ke ano o ka Helu 11: Master Number numerology

ʻO ka helu 11 kekahi o nā helu kumu hanohano ʻekolu ma ka helu helu—me ka ʻaoʻao 22 a me 33. Ma nā ʻōnaehana kuʻuna, ʻaʻole i hoʻemi ʻia i ka huahelu hoʻokahi no ka mea e lawe ana i kahi haʻalulu kiʻekiʻe a intuitive. ʻO ka poʻe me ke Ala Ola Helu 11 i manaʻo pinepine ʻia he poʻe ʻimi hohonu me kahi alapine ikaika kūʻokoʻa i kēia ola.

---

## He aha ka helu kumu?

Lawe ʻia nā helu kumu (11, 22, a me 33) i kahi huahelu pālua o nā helu like, e hōʻike ana i kahi haʻalulu paʻa paʻa loa. Lawe ʻo 11 i ka haʻalulu pālua o 1, lawe ʻo 22 i kahi haʻalulu pālua o 2, a ʻo 33 lawe i kahi haʻalulu pālua o 3. Hōʻike kēia resonance ʻelua i kahi hiki ke kiʻekiʻe no ka ʻike, i hui pū ʻia me nā haʻawina ola kikoʻī e paipai i ka hoʻopaʻi pilikino hohonu.

## Helu 11 ma ka Pili a me ke Kulike

Ma nā pilina, ʻimi ka poʻe me ka helu 11 i nā pilina ʻoiaʻiʻo, hohonu, a me ka ʻoiaʻiʻo. Hoʻonui maoli lākou i ka wā e mahalo ai kā lākou hoa i ko lākou naʻau kiʻekiʻe a me nā hiʻohiʻona kūʻokoʻa i ke ola. Kūleʻa a mālama nui, ulu lākou me nā hoa hana e hāʻawi i ka paʻa a me ke kumu o ka naʻau. ʻIke pinepine ʻia kā lākou hui helu nui me nā helu 2, 6, a me nā helu kumu ʻē aʻe.
