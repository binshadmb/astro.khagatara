# Heluhelu numerology manuahi: E ʻike i kāu Cosmic Blueprint

ʻO numerology kekahi o nā ʻōnaehana ʻike pilikino kahiko loa o ka honua. No nā kaukani makahiki, ua hoʻohana nā moʻomeheu a puni ka honua i nā helu e hoʻomaopopo i ke ʻano, ka pilina, a me ke kahe kūlohelohe o ke ola. I kēia lā, hiki iā ʻoe ke helu i kou ala ola a me nā helu cosmic me ka manuahi me kā mākou ʻōnaehana numerology chaldean a me Védic.

---

## He aha ka helu ala ola?

ʻO kāu Helu Alanui Ola ka helu koʻikoʻi loa i kāu pakuhi numerology. Loaʻa ia mai nā helu pololei o kou lā hānau. Ke hana nei kēia helu ma ke aniani ʻuhane, e hōʻike ana i kou ʻano pilikino, kāu mau talena kūlohelohe, a me nā haʻawina ʻuhane āu i koho ai e makaʻala i kēia ola.

---

## Pehea e helu ai i kou helu ala ola

No ka helu ʻana i kāu Helu Ala Ola, e hōʻuluʻulu ʻoe i nā huahelu a pau o kou lā hānau (lā, mahina, a me ka makahiki) a hoʻemi iā lākou i hoʻokahi huahelu ma waena o 1 a me 9.

Eia naʻe, inā like kāu huina**11, 22, a i ʻole 33**, mai hoemi ia lakou. Kapa ʻia kēia mau mea**Helu Kumu**a hōʻike i nā haʻalulu kiʻekiʻe loa a me ka noʻonoʻo ʻana iā ia iho.

### Laʻana ʻanuʻu
Inā hānau ʻoe ma Iune 13, 1981:
1.**E ho'ēmi i ka lā:**13 → 1 + 3 = 4
2.**E hoemi i ka mahina:**ʻO Iune ka mahina 6 → 6
3.**E hoemi i ka makahiki:**1981 → 1 + 9 + 8 + 1 = 19 → 1 + 9 = 10 → 1 + 0 = 1
4.**E houluulu i na huina:**4 (La) + 6 (Mahina) + 1 (Makahiki) = 11
*Ka hopena:**Alanui Ola 11**— Ka Haku Intuitive.*

## ʻO Védic vs. Western numerology

ʻOiai kaulana loa ka Western numerology (pythagorean),**Védic numerology**(i hoʻokumu ʻia ma India kahiko) hoʻohui pololei i nā helu me ka hele ʻana o ka mahina astronomical.

Ma mua o ka nānā 'ana i nā helu ma kahi ka'awale, ho'ohui ka Védic i kou lā hānau me kāu Moon Sign (**rashi**), Hoku Hanau (**nakshatra**), a me ka pōʻaiapuni honua o kēia manawa (**dasha**). Hāʻawi kēia i kahi hiʻohiʻona piha, hohonu, a me ka lokahi o kāu alignment ikaika.
