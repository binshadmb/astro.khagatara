# Chate ea Bonohe ba Linaleli ea Mahala: Tseba Tsela e Hao ea Mahlakore

Chate ea hao ea bolepi ba linaleli e feta hōle lethathamo le bonolo la lipolanete le matšoao. Ke puo ea lipalo ea maholimo e ngotsoeng ka nako e nepahetseng ea tsoalo ea hau. Védic Astrology (Jyotish), e tsoang India ea khale, e fana ka e 'ngoe ea litlhaloso tsa lefats'e tse hlakileng le tse tebileng tsa moea tsa 'mapa ona oa bokahohle.

---

## Chate ea Bonohe ba linaleli ea Védic ke eng?

Chate ea Bonohe ba linaleli ea Védic, eo ka tloaelo e tsejoang e le a**Kundali**kapa**chate ea tsoalo**, ke 'mapa o phethahetseng oa geometri oa maemo a lipolanete ka nako ea hau ea tsoalo. Ho fapana le linaleli tsa Bophirimela, Jyotish e sebelisa**zodiac e ka thoko**—maemo a sebele a lipolanete le lihlopha tsa linaleli sepakapakeng kajeno—e leng se etsang hore e be e nepahetseng haholo.

---

## Lipolanete tse 9 tsa Védic (Grahas)

Ho Jyotish, 'mele ea lipolanete tse robong e emela likarolo tsa mantlha tsa kelello le matla a rona:

*   **Surya (Letsatsi):**Moya, boetapele, morero, le matla a ka hare.
*   **Chandra (Khoeli):**Kelello, maikutlo, kholiso ea bo-mme, le intuition.
*   **Mangala (Mars):**Matla, ho khanna, sebete, ketso, le matla a 'mele.
*   **Buddha (Mercury):**Bohlale, puisano, ho hlaka ha tlhahlobo, le khoebo.
*   **Guru (Jupiter):**Bohlale, kholo ea moea, katoloso, thuto, le mohau.
*   **Shukra (Venus):**Lerato, kutloano ea bonono, likamano, ntlafatso, le botle.
*   **Shani (Saturn):**Khalemelo, mamello, boikarabello, le sebopeho.
*   **Rahu (Node e ka Leboea):**Litakatso tsa mantsoe, lithuto tsa lintho tse bonahalang, boqapi le katoloso.
*   **Ketu (Node e ka Boroa):**Ho lokolloa ka hare, ho arohana moeeng, metso e fetileng, le tokoloho.

## Sidereal vs. Tropical Zodiac Accuracy

Bonohe ba linaleli Bophirimela bo sebelisa**linaleli tsa tropike**, e lokiselitsoeng linako tsa selemo. Védic Astrology e sebelisa**zodiac e ka thoko**, e itšetlehileng ka boemo ba sebele ba linaleli.

Ka lebaka la selelekela sa litekanyo, hajoale ho na le phapang ea likhato tse ka bang 23 lipakeng tsa litsamaiso tsena tse peli. Ke kahoo Védic Moon Sign kapa Ascendant ea hau e atisang ho fapana le letšoao la hau la Bophirimela-ho lumellana ka ho toba le 'nete ea sebele ea leholimo bosiu.

## Ho Utloisisa Lipotoloho Tsa Nako ea Hao e Matla

E 'ngoe ea lintlha tsa bohlokoahali tsa chate ea Védic ke bokhoni ba eona ba ho etsa 'mapa oa potoloho ea matla ka nako. Re sebelisa Vimshottari Dasha le lipalangoang tsa lipolanete, re ka utloisisa linako tsa lipolanete tse amang mehopolo ea hau, bophelo bo botle le likamano tsa hau, ho fana ka seipone se setle bakeng sa ho itlhokomela le ho phela ka kelello.
