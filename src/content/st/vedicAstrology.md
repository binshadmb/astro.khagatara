# Védic Astrology ea Mahala: Leseli la Jyotish Marang-rang

Jyotish—Védic Astrology—ka tloaelo e tsejoa e le "saense ea leseli." Ka lilemo tse fetang 5,000 tsa histori e sa tsitsang, ke e 'ngoe ea mekhoa e rarahaneng ka ho fetisisa ea ho itlhalosa e entsoeng ke batho. Sebakeng sa ho hlalosa litšobotsi tse akaretsang tsa botho, Jyotish o hlahloba mekhoa e tebileng ea bokahohle le linako tse thusang ho tlisa kutloano bophelong ba hau.

---

## Jyotish ke eng?

Jyotish ke lentsoe la Sanskrit le bolelang "saense ea leseli" kapa "saense ea lihloliloeng tsa leholimo." Ke e ’ngoe ea Li-Vedanga tse tšeletseng—e leng lithupelo tse thusang tsa Vedas, libuka tse halalelang tsa boholo-holo. Jyotish e ile ea qaptjoa ke bo-Rishi—liboni tse bohlale tsa India ea boholo-holo—ba ileng ba bona mekhoa e phethahetseng ea lipalo ea bokahohle ’me ba hlokomela kamano ea bona le kutloisiso ea motho.

---

## Makala a Mararo a Jyotish

Saense e kholo ea Jyotish e arotsoe ka litšiea tse tharo tsa mantlha:

1.**Ganita (Astronomi le Palo):**Motheo oa lipalo. E lekanya hantle hore na linaleli le lipolanete li eme hokae ka ho sebelisa linaleli tse ka thōko—e leng boemo ba sebele ba lihlopha tsa linaleli tse sepakapakeng kajeno.
2.**Hora (Thuto ea Natala):**Tlhahlobo ea chate ea tsoalo ea motho ka mong. E ithuta hore na lipolanete li tsamaisana joang le semelo sa motho, matla le tsela ea hae ea bophelo.
3.**Samhita (Thuto ea Mundane):**Boithuto ba liketsahalo tse kopaneng, lipaterone tsa tlhaho, le potoloho e pharaletseng ea tsoelopele ea batho.

## The 27 Nakshatras: Lunar Mansions

Nakshatra ke matlo a maholo a khoeli a 27 a Védic Astrology—lihlopha tsa linaleli tseo Khoeli e tsamaeang ho tsona ha e potoloha khoeli le khoeli. Tsoalo ea hau ea Nakshatra ke e 'ngoe ea likarolo tsa bohlokoahali tsa chate ea hau, e emelang boemo ba kelello ba hau:

*   **Ashwini:**Lebelo, matla a pholiso, le qaleho e ncha.
*   **Bharani:**Phetoho e tebileng le nchafatso.
*   **Krittika:**Ho hloekisa maikutlo, ho hlaka, le taeo.
*   **Rohini:**Boqapi, botle, le mofuthu oa maikutlo.
*   **Morigashira:**Bohelehele, ho batlisisa, le boithuto bo hlokolosi.
*   **Ardra:**Ho hloekisa lifefo le nchafatso ea boqapi.
*   **Punarvasu:**Tsosoloso, tšireletso, le qaleho e ncha.
*   **Pushya:**Phepo ea moea, bohlale le tlhokomelo.
*   **Ashlesha:**Liphiri, botebo, le matla a ka hare a tsepamisitsoeng.
*   *(Le li-Nakshatra tse ling tse 18 tse ntle, e 'ngoe le e 'ngoe e na le matla a eona a ikhethang a ho sisinyeha)*

## Vimshottari Dasha: Lipotoloho tsa Lipolanete

Vimshottari Dasha ke sistimi e tšepahalang ka ho fetesisa ea nako ea lipolanete ho Jyotish. E talima potoloho e felletseng ea bophelo ba motho ea lilemo tse 120 e le tatellano ea linako tsa lipolanete, e 'ngoe le e' ngoe e laoloa ke a mang a matla a robong a lipolanete:

*   **Ketu (Lilemo tse 7):**Ho nahanisisa ka moea, ho thuisa ka hare, le metso ea nakong e fetileng.
*   **Venus (Lilemo tse 20):**Kutloano ea boqapi, likamano, le ntlafatso.
*   **Letsatsi (Lilemo tse 6):**Matla a ka hare, tsepamiso, morero, le ho hlaka.
*   **Khoeli (Lilemo tse 10):**Kholiso ea maikutlo, kamohelo, le metso ea malapa.
*   **Mars (lilemo tse 7):**Matla a bohlokoa, ho tsepamisa maikutlo, sebete le ketso.
*   **Rahu (Lilemo tse 18):**Takatso ea maemo, maemo a kantle ho naha, le lithuto tsa lefatše.
*   **Jupiter (Lilemo tse 16):**Bohlale, khōlo ea moea, katoloso, le ho ithuta.
*   **Saturn (Lilemo tse 19):**Khalemelo, boikarabelo, mamello, le thuto e tiileng.
*   **Mercury (Lilemo tse 17):**Ho hlaka hoa kelello, khoebo, le puisano.
