## Sidereal vs. Tropical Zodiac Accuracy

Western Astrology uses the **tropical zodiac**, which is fixed to the seasons of the year. Védic Astrology uses the **sidereal zodiac**, which is anchored to the actual astronomical positions of the stars.

Due to the precesión of the equinoxes, there is currently a difference of approximately 23 degrees between the two systems. This is why your Védic Moon Sign or Ascendant is often different from your Western sign—aligning directly with the physical reality of the night sky.
