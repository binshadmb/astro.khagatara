# Name Numerology: Discover the Power of Your Name

Your name was not chosen by accident. According to ancient numerology, every letter in your name carries a specific vibrational frequency that influences your personality, relationships, and life path. The Chaldean system, utilized in Védic Numerology, assigns numerical values to each letter based on its sound vibration—rather than just its position in the alphabet.

---

## The Chaldean System of Numerology

The Chaldean system is one of the world's most ancient numerological methods, originating in Mesopotamia more than 4,000 years ago. While the Pythagorean system simply numbers letters from 1 to 9 sequentially, the Chaldean system assigns values based on sound frequency, omitting the number 9 as it was considered sacred:

*   **1:** A, I, J, Q, Y
*   **2:** B, K, R
*   **3.**C, G, L, S
*   **4.**D, M, T
*   **5.**E, H, N, X
*   **6.**U, V, W
*   **7**‘O
*   **8**F
