## What is Jyotish?

Jyotish is a Sanskrit word meaning "science of light" or "science of celestial bodies." It is one of the six Vedangas—the auxiliary disciplines of the Vedas, the ancient sacred texts. Jyotish was developed by the Rishis—wise seers of ancient India—who observed the perfect mathematical patterns of the cosmos and realized their correlation with human consciousness.

---

## The Three Branches of Jyotish

The vast science of Jyotish is divided into three core pillars:

1. **Ganita (Astronomy and Calculation):** The mathematical foundation. It calculates the exact positions of the stars and planets using the sidereal zodiac—the actual physical positions of the constellations in the sky today.
2. **Hora (Natal Study):** The analysis of the individual birth chart. It studies how the planetary alignments at birth correspond to an individual's character, strengths, and life path.
3. **Samhita (Mundane Study):** The study of collective events, natural patterns, and the broad cycles of human civilizations.

## The Vimshottari Dasha: Planetary Cycles

The Vimshottari Dasha is the most widely trusted system of planetary periods in Jyotish. It views a complete potential human life cycle of 120 years as a sequence of planetary periods, each governed by one of the nine planetary energies:

*   **Ketu (7 Years):** Spiritual contemplation, inner reflection, and past roots.
*   **Venus (20 Years):** Creative harmony, relationships, and refinement.
*   **Sun (6 Years):** Inner strength, focus, purpose, and clarity.
*   **Moon (10 Years):** Emotional nurturing, receptivity, and family roots.
*   **Mars (7 Years):** Vital energy, focus, courage, and action.
*   **Rahu (18 Years):** Ambition, foreign horizons, and worldly lessons.
*   **Jupiter (16 Years):** Wisdom, spiritual growth, expansion, and study.
*   **Saturn (19 Years):** Discipline, responsibility, patience, and solid learning.
*   **Mercury (17 Years):** Intellectual clarity, commerce, and communication.
