# Yemahara Védic Astrology: Chiedza cheJyotish Online

Jyotish-Védic Astrology-inowanzozivikanwa se "sainzi yechiedza." Neanopfuura makore 5,000 enhorondo isina kuparara, ndiyo imwe yenzira dzakaomarara dzekuzvinzwisisa dzakagadzirwa nevanhu. Panzvimbo pekungotsanangura hunhu hwakajairwa, Jyotish inoongorora yakadzika cosmic mapatani uye nguva kutenderera kunobatsira kuunza kuwirirana kuhupenyu hwako.

---

## Chii chinonzi Jyotish?

Jyotish ishoko reSanskrit rinoreva "sainzi yechiedza" kana "sainzi yezvitunha zvekudenga." Ndiyo imwe yeVedangas mitanhatu—zvidzidzo zvebetsero zveVedas, zvinyorwa zvinoyera zvekare. Jyotish yakagadzirwa nevaRishis — vaoni vakachenjera veIndia yekare — avo vakacherekedza maitiro akakwana emasvomhu epasi uye akaziva kuwirirana kwawo nekuziva kwevanhu.

---

## Matavi Matatu eJyotish

Sainzi yakakura yeJyotish yakakamurwa kuita mbiru nhatu dzepakati:

1.**Ganita (Astronomy uye Calculation):**The masvomhu hwaro. Inoverenga nzvimbo chaidzo dzenyeredzi nemapuraneti ichishandisa sidereal zodiac - iwo chaiwo nzvimbo dzemuviri dzemapoka enyeredzi ari mudenga nhasi.
2.**Hora (Chidzidzo cheNatal):**Kuongororwa kwechati yekuzvarwa kwega. Inodzidza kuti kurongeka kwepuraneti pakuzvarwa kunoenderana sei nehunhu hwemunhu, simba, uye nzira yehupenyu.
3.**Samhita (Chidzidzo cheMundane):**Chidzidzo chezviitiko zvakabatanidzwa, maitiro echisikigo, uye kutenderera kwakafara kwebudiriro yevanhu.

## Iwo 27 Nakshatras: Lunar Mansions

MaNakshatra ndiwo dzimba 27 dzemwedzi dzeVédic Astrology—mapoka enyeredzi ayo Mwedzi unofamba nawo mukutenderera kwawo kwemwedzi. Kuzvarwa kwako Nakshatra ndechimwe chezvinhu zvakakosha pane chati yako, inomiririra mamiriro epfungwa dzako:

*   **Ashwini:**Kukurumidza, simba rekuporesa, uye kutanga kutsva.
*   **Bharani:**Shanduko yakadzika uye kuvandudzwa.
*   **Krittika:**Kuchenesa kutarisa, kujeka, uye chirango.
*   **Rohini:**Kusika, runako, uye kudziya kwemanzwiro.
*   **Mrigashira:**Kuda kuziva, kutsvaga, uye kuongorora kwakadzama.
*   **Ardra:**Kuchenesa madutu nekuvandudza kwekugadzira.
*   **Punarvasu:**Kudzoreredza, kudzivirira, uye kutanga kutsva.
*   **Pushya:**Zvokudya zvomudzimu, uchenjeri, uye kutarisira.
*   **Ashlesha:**Chakavanzika, kudzika, uye kutarisisa simba remukati.
*   *(Uye gumi nesere mamwe maNakshatra akanaka, imwe neimwe yakatakura simba rayo rekuzunguza)*

## The Vimshottari Dasha: Planetary Cycles

Iyo Vimshottari Dasha ndiyo inonyanya kuvimbika system yenguva dzemapuraneti muJyotish. Inoona yakakwana yehupenyu hwehupenyu hwemunhu hwemakore 120 senhevedzano yenguva dzemapuraneti, imwe neimwe inotongwa nerimwe remasimba mapfumbamwe epasi:

*   **Ketu (7 Makore):**Kufungisisa kwemweya, kufungisisa kwemukati, uye midzi yapfuura.
*   **Venus (Makore makumi maviri):**Creative kuwirirana, hukama, uye kunatsiridza.
*   **Zuva (Makore matanhatu):**Simba remukati, kutarisa, chinangwa, uye kujeka.
*   **Mwedzi (Makore gumi):**Kukurudzira manzwiro, kugamuchira, uye midzi yemhuri.
*   **Mars (7 Makore):**Simba rinokosha, kutarisa, ushingi, uye chiito.
*   **Rahu (18 Makore):**Chishuvo, maonero ekunze, uye zvidzidzo zvenyika.
*   **Jupiter (16 Makore):**Uchenjeri, kukura pamweya, kuwedzera, uye kudzidza.
*   **Saturn (19 Makore):**Kuranga, mutoro, moyo murefu, uye kudzidza kwakasimba.
*   **Mercury (17 Makore):**Kujeka kwenjere, kutengeserana, uye kutaurirana.
