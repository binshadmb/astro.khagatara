## Chii chinonzi Hupenyu Nzira Nhamba?

Hupenyu Hwako Hwenzira Nhamba ndiyo inonyanya kukosha nhamba mune yako manhamba chati. Inobva panhamba chaidzo dzezuva rako rekuzvarwa. Nhamba iyi inoita segirazi remweya, inoratidza hunhu hwako hwepamusoro, matarenda ako echisikigo, uye zvidzidzo zvepamweya zvawakasarudza kuongorora muhupenyu huno.

## Maitiro ekuverenga Hupenyu Hwako Nzira Nhamba

Kuti uverenge Hupenyu HweNhamba Hwako, unopeta manhamba ese ezuva rako rekuzvarwa (zuva, mwedzi, uye gore) uye wodzidzikisa kusvika kune imwe nhamba pakati pe1 ne9.

Nekudaro, kana mari yako yakaenzana**11, 22, kana 33**, musavaderedza. Aya anonzi**Master Numbers**uye inomiririra kuzunguzika kwakakwirira uye kwakadzama kuzviona.

### Nhanho-ne-Nhanho Muenzaniso
Kana iwe wakazvarwa munaJune 13, 1981:
1.**Deredza Zuva:**13 → 1 + 3 = 4
2.**Deredza Mwedzi:**Chikumi mwedzi wechitanhatu → 6
3.**Deredza Gore:**1981 → 1 + 9 + 8 + 1 = 19 → 1 + 9 = 10 → 1 + 0 = 1
4.**Pfupikidza Zvose:**4 (Zuva) + 6 (Mwedzi) + 1 (Gore) = 11

## Védic vs. Western Numerology

Nepo Western Numerology (Pythagorean) ichizivikanwa zvakanyanya,**Védic Numerology**(yakabva kuIndia yekare) inobatanidza nhamba zvakananga nemafambisirwo emwedzi wenyeredzi.

Panzvimbo pekutarisa nhamba uri wega, nzira yeVédic inobatanidza zuva rako rekuzvarwa neChiratidzo chako cheMwedzi (**Rashi**), Nyeredzi yekuzvarwa (**Nakshatra**), uye kutenderera kwemapuraneti kwazvino (**Dasha**) Izvi zvinopa yakanyanya kuzara yakazara, yakadzama, uye inowirirana maonero ekurongeka kwako kwakasimba.
