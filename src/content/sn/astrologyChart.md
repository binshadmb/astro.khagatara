## Chii chinonzi Védic Astrology Chati?

A Védic Astrology Chati, yagara ichizivikanwa sea**Kundali**kana**chati yekuzvarwa**, imepu yejometri yakakwana yenzvimbo dzepasi panguva yako chaiyo yekuzvarwa. ## Iwo 9 Védic Planets (Grahas)

MuJyotish, iyo mipfumbamwe miviri yepasi inomiririra zvakakosha zvemukati mekuziva uye simba:

*   **Surya (Zuva):**Mweya, hutungamiriri, chinangwa, uye simba remukati.
*   **Chandra (Mwedzi):**Pfungwa, manzwiro, kurera amai, uye intuition.
*   **Mangala (Mars):**Simba, kutyaira, ushingi, chiito, uye simba remuviri.
*   **Bhudha (Mercury):**Hungwaru, kutaurirana, kujekesa kwekuongorora, uye kutengeserana.
*   **Guru (Jupiter):**Uchenjeri, kukura pamweya, kuwedzera, kudzidza, nenyasha.
*   **Shukra (Venus):**Rudo, kuwirirana kwehunyanzvi, hukama, kukwenenzvera, uye runako.
*   **Shani (Saturn):**Kuranga, mwoyo murefu, mutoro, uye chimiro.
*   **Rahu (North Node):**Zvishuwo zveshoko, zvidzidzo zvenyama, hunyanzvi, uye kuwedzera.
*   **Ketu (South Node):**Kusunungurwa kwemukati, kusungwa kwemweya, midzi yakapfuura, uye kusunungurwa.

## Sidereal vs. Tropical Zodiac Accuracy

Western Astrology inoshandisa iyo**tropical zodiac**, iyo inotarirwa mwaka wegore. Védic Astrology inoshandisa iyo**sidereal zodiac**, iyo inosungirirwa panzvimbo chaiyo yenyeredzi yenyeredzi.

Nekuda kwe precesión ye equinoxes, parizvino pane musiyano weanosvika madhigirii makumi maviri nematatu pakati pezvirongwa zviviri izvi. Ichi ndicho chikonzero chako Védic Moon Sign kana Ascendant inowanzosiyana nechiratidzo chako chekuMadokero-chichienderana zvakananga nehuchokwadi hwekudenga hweusiku.
