## Wanem em i wanpela Laip Rot Namba?

Laip Rot Namba bilong yu em i namba we i bikpela samting tru insait long 'numerology chart' bilong yu. Em i kam long ol stretpela namba bilong de bilong bon bilong yu. Dispela namba i wok olsem wanpela mira bilong spirit, na i soim ol as pasin bilong yu, ol gutpela save bilong yu, na ol spesifik spiritual leson yu bin makim long lainim long dispela laiptaim.

## Hau long Kalkuletim Laip Rot Namba Bilong Yu

Long skelim Laip Rot Namba bilong yu, yu mas bungim olgeta namba bilong de bilong bon bilong yu (de, mun, na yia) na daunim i go long wanpela namba namel long 1 na 9.

Tasol, sapos namba bilong yu i wankain olsem**11, 22, o 33**, noken daunim ol. Ol i kolim ol dispela**Ol bikpela namba**na i makim ol 'vibrations' we i antap tru na 'self-reflection' i go insait tru.

### Step-bai-Step Eksampel
Sapos yu bin bon long Jun 13, 1981:
1.**Daunim De:**13 → 1 + 3 = 4
2.**Daunim mun:**Jun em i namba 6 mun → 6
3.**Daunim Yia:**1981 → 1 + 9 + 8 + 1 = 19 → 1 + 9 = 10 → 1 + 0 = 1
4.**Bungim ol Total:**4 (De) + 6 (Mun) + 1 (Yia) = 11
*Risal:**Laip Rot 11**— The Master Intuitive.*

## Védic vs. Western numerology

Taim Western numerology (pythagorean) em i bikpela samting,**Védic numerology**(i kam long India bilong bipo) i bungim ol namba stret wantaim ol 'astronomical moon transit'.

Noken lukluk long ol namba wan wan, Védic rot i bungim deit bilong bon bilong yu wantaim Mun Sain bilong yu (**rashi**), Birth Star (**nakshatra**), na 'current planetary cycle' (**dasha**). Dispela i givim wanpela gutpela, dip, na gutpela lukluk long strong bilong yu.
