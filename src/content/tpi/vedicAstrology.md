# Fri Védic astrology: Lait bilong jyotish Online

jyotish - Védic astrology - ol i save kolim olsem "saiens bilong lait." Wantaim moa long 5,000 yia bilong histori we i no bruk, em i wanpela bilong ol gutpela sistem bilong kliagut long yu yet we ol manmeri i bin kamapim. jyotish i no stori tasol long ol pasin bilong man, em i lukluk long ol dip kosmik paten na taim saikel we i helpim long bringim gutpela sindaun long laip bilong yu.

---

## jyotish em i wanem samting?

jyotish em i wanpela tok Sanskrit we i min olsem "saiens bilong lait" o "saiens bilong ol samting bilong skai." Em i wanpela bilong 6-pela Vedangas - ol narapela samting bilong Vedas, em olpela rait holi. jyotish em ol Rishis i bin kamapim - ol saveman bilong India bilong bipo - husat i bin lukluk long ol gutpela matematikel paten bilong kosmos na luksave long wok bilong ol wantaim tingting bilong ol man.

---

## Tripela han bilong jyotish

Bikpela saiens bilong jyotish i bruk i go long tripela bikpela samting:

1.**Ganita (Astronomi na Kalkulesen):**As tingting bilong matematiks. Em i save skelim stretpela ples bilong ol sta na planet long rot bilong 'sideal zodiac' - em i ples tru bilong ol lain sta long skai tude.
2.**Hora (Natal Study):**Skelim bilong wanwan 'birth chart'. Em i stadim hau ol planet i stap long taim mama i karim man i stret wantaim pasin, strong bilong em, na rot bilong laip bilong em.
3.**Samhita (Mundane Study):**Stadi bilong ol samting i kamap wantaim, ol samting i kamap long ples, na ol bikpela samting i save kamap long ol manmeri.

## Ol 27 nakshatras: Lunar Mansions

Ol nakshatras em ol 27 bikpela haus bilong mun bilong Védic astrology - ol lain sta we Mun i save raun long en long olgeta mun. Birth nakshatra bilong yu em i wanpela bikpela samting tru insait long jat bilong yu, we i makim tingting bilong yu:

*   **Ashwini:**Spid, strong bilong oraitim sik, na nupela stat.
*   **Bharani:**Dippela senis na nupela.
*   **Krittika:**Mekim tingting i kamap klin, klia, na disiplin.
*   **Rohini:**Pasin bilong kamapim ol nupela samting, naispela, na gutpela tingting.
*   **Mrigashira:**Pasin bilong laik save, painim samting, na wok painimaut gut.
*   **Ardra:**Klinim ol bikpela win na kamapim nupela tingting.
*   **Punarvasu:**Restoresen, proteksen, na nupela stat.
*   **Pushya:**Kaikai long sait bilong spirit, savetingting, na lukaut.
*   **Ashlesha:**Tok hait, dip, na strong insait long yu.
*   *(Na 18 moa naispela nakshatras, we olgeta wan wan i gat narakain strong bilong en)*

## vimshottari dasha: Planetary Cycles

vimshottari dasha em i nambawan sistem bilong ol taim bilong ol planet insait long jyotish. Em i lukluk long wanpela ful potensel laip saikel bilong 120 yia olsem wanpela lain bilong ol planet taim, we wanwan i stap aninit long wanpela bilong 9-pela planet pawa:

*   **Ketu (7-pela krismas):**Tingting long ol samting bilong spirit, tingting insait long bel, na ol as bilong bipo.
*   **Venus (20 Yia):**Gutpela pasin, rilesensip, na gutpela pasin.
*   **San (6-pela Yia):**Strong bilong bel, tingting, as tingting, na klia tingting.
*   **Mun (10-pela Yia):**Pasin bilong lukautim gut bel, pasin bilong kisim ol samting, na ol as bilong famili.
*   **Mars (7 Yia):**Vital strong, lukluk, strongpela bel, na eksen.
*   **Rahu (18 Yia):**Ambisen, ol narapela tingting, na ol leson bilong dispela graun.
*   **Jupita (16 Yia):**Gutpela tingting, gro long sait bilong spirit, go bikpela, na stadi.
*   **Saturn (19 Yia):**Disiplin, risponsibiliti, pasin bilong stap isi, na strongpela lainim.
*   **Mercury (17 Yia):**Klia tingting, wok bisnis, na toktok.

Em i wanpela ditel, trupela kosmik mep we ol i bin kalkuletim wantaim matematikel presis we ol i yusim ol trupela astronomikal mun transit na ol ancestral Védic na chaldean fomula. As tingting bilong mipela em long givim yu klia tingting, rispek, na wanpela gutpela tul bilong painimaut yu yet na tingting gut.
