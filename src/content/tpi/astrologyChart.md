## Sidereal vs. Tropical Zodiac Accuracy

Ol saveman bilong ol sta bilong Wes i save yusim**tropikel zodiak**, we i stap long ol sisen bilong yia. Védic astrology i yusim**sidereal zodiac**, we i stap long ples tru bilong ol sta.

Bikos long 'precesión' bilong ol 'equinoxes', nau i gat wanpela samting i narapela long 23 digri namel long tupela sistem. Dispela em i as na Védic Moon Sign o ascendant bilong yu i save narapela kain long Western sain bilong yu - i stret wantaim lukluk bilong skai long nait.
