# I-Védic Astrology yamahhala: Ukukhanya kwe-Jyotish Online

I-Jyotish—Védic Astrology—ngokwesiko yaziwa ngokuthi “isayensi yokukhanya.” Ngeminyaka engaphezu kuka-5,000 yomlando ongashintshiwe, ingenye yezinhlelo eziyinkimbinkimbi kakhulu zokuziqonda ezithuthukiswe isintu. Kunokuba achaze nje izici zobuntu ezijwayelekile, u-Jyotish uhlola amaphethini e-cosmic ajulile nemijikelezo yesikhathi esiza ukuletha ukuzwana empilweni yakho.

---

## Yini i-Jyotish?

I-Jyotish igama lesiSanskrit elisho "isayensi yokukhanya" noma "isayensi yezidalwa zasezulwini." Ingenye yamaVedanga ayisithupha—izifundo ezisizayo zeVedas, imibhalo engcwele yasendulo. I-Jyotish yasungulwa abakwaRishi—ababoni abahlakaniphile baseNdiya yasendulo—ababona amaphethini aphelele ezibalo zendawo yonke futhi baqaphela ukuhlobana kwabo nokwazi komuntu.

---

## Amagatsha Amathathu aseJyotish

Isayensi enkulu yeJyotish ihlukaniswe yaba izinsika ezintathu eziyinhloko:

1.**I-Ganita (Isayensi yezinkanyezi nokubala):**Isisekelo sezibalo. Ibala ngqo ukuma kwezinkanyezi namaplanethi kusetshenziswa i-sidereal zodiac—izindawo ezingokoqobo zemilaza esibhakabhakeni namuhla.
2.**I-Hora (Isifundo SaseNatali):**Ukuhlaziywa kweshadi lokuzalwa ngakunye. Icwaninga ukuthi ukuhleleka kwamaplanethi ngesikhathi sokuzalwa kuhambelana kanjani nomlingiswa womuntu, amandla, nendlela yokuphila.
3.**I-Samhita (Isifundo Sase-Mundane):**Ucwaningo lwezehlakalo ezihlangene, amaphethini emvelo, nemijikelezo ebanzi yempucuko yabantu.

## AmaNakshatra angama-27: Izindlu ZaseLunar

I-Nakshatras iyizithabathaba zenyanga ezingu-27 ze-Védic Astrology—amaqoqo iNyanga ehamba ngawo emzileni wayo wanyanga zonke. Ukuzalwa kwakho i-Nakshatra ingenye yezinto ezibaluleke kakhulu zeshadi lakho, emele isimo semvelo somqondo wakho:

*   **Ashwini:**Ijubane, amandla okuphulukisa, neziqalo ezintsha.
*   **I-Bharani:**Ukuguqulwa okujulile nokuvuselela.
*   **I-Krittika:**Ukuhlanza ukugxila, ukucaca, nokuziphatha.
*   **I-Rohini:**Ukudala, ubuhle, nokufudumala kwemizwa.
*   **UMrigashira:**Ilukuluku, ukusesha, nokuhlola okubucayi.
*   **I-Ardra:**Ukuhlanza izivunguvungu kanye nokwenza kabusha kokudala.
*   **I-Punarvasu:**Ukubuyiselwa, ukuvikelwa, neziqalo ezintsha.
*   **Pushya:**Ukudla okungokomoya, ukuhlakanipha, nokunakekelwa.
*   **I-Ashlesha:**Imfihlakalo, ukujula, namandla angaphakathi agxilile.
*   *(Kanye namaNakshatra amahle angu-18, ngalinye liphethe amandla alo ahlukile okudlidliza)*

## I-Vimshottari Dasha: Imijikelezo Yeplanethi

I-Vimshottari Dasha iwuhlelo oluthenjwa kakhulu kunazo zonke lwezikhathi zeplanethi e-Jyotish. Ibuka umjikelezo wokuphila komuntu ongaba khona ophelele weminyaka eyi-120 njengokulandelana kwezikhathi zeplanethi, ngayinye ebuswa amandla awodwa weplanethi eyisishiyagalolunye:

*   **I-Ketu (Iminyaka engu-7):**Ukuzindla okungokomoya, ukuzindla kwangaphakathi, kanye nezimpande zesikhathi esidlule.
*   **I-Venus (Iminyaka engu-20):**Ukuvumelana kokudala, ubudlelwano, nokucwengwa.
*   **Ilanga (Iminyaka engu-6):**Amandla angaphakathi, ukugxila, inhloso, nokucaca.
*   **Inyanga (Iminyaka eyi-10):**Ukunakekela imizwa, ukwamukela, nezimpande zomndeni.
*   **I-Mars (iminyaka engu-7):**Amandla abalulekile, ukugxila, isibindi, kanye nesenzo.
*   **URahu (Iminyaka engu-18):**Isifiso sokuvelela, ama-horizons angaphandle, nezifundo zezwe.
*   **I-Jupiter (Iminyaka engu-16):**Ukuhlakanipha, ukukhula ngokomoya, ukwanda, nokufunda.
*   **ISaturn (Iminyaka engu-19):**Isiyalo, umthwalo wemfanelo, ukubekezela, nokufunda okuqinile.
*   **I-Mercury (iminyaka engu-17):**Ukucaca kobuhlakani, ukuhweba, nokuxhumana.
