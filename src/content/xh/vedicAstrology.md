# I-Védic Astrology yasimahla: Ukukhanya kweJyotish kwi-Intanethi

IJyotish-Védic Astrology-iyaziwa ngokuba "yisayensi yokukhanya." Ngaphezulu kweminyaka engama-5,000 yembali engaqhawukiyo, yenye yezona nkqubo zintsonkothileyo zokuziqonda eziphuhliswe luluntu. Endaweni yokuchaza nje iimpawu zobuntu ngokubanzi, uJyotish uphonononga iipateni ezinzulu zecosmic kunye nomjikelo wexesha onceda ukuzisa imvisiswano ebomini bakho.

---

## Yintoni iJyotish?

I-Jyotish ligama lesiSanskrit elithetha "isayensi yokukhanya" okanye "inzululwazi yendalo yasezulwini." Iyenye yeeVedanga ezintandathu—iincwadi ezincedisayo zeVedas, imibhalo engcwele yamandulo. IJyotish yaphuhliswa ngabakwaRishi—ababoni abalumkileyo baseIndiya yamandulo—ababona iipatheni ezigqibeleleyo zemathematika zendalo iphela baza baqonda ukunxulumana kwazo nokuqonda komntu.

---

## Amasebe amathathu eJyotish

Isayensi enkulu yeJyotish yahlulwe yaba ziintsika ezintathu eziphambili:

1.**I-Ganita (i-Astronomy kunye nokubala):**Isiseko semathematika. Ibala iindawo ezichanekileyo zeenkwenkwezi kunye neeplanethi zisebenzisa i-zodiac esecaleni-ezona zikhundla ezibonakalayo zeenkwenkwezi ezisesibhakabhakeni namhlanje.
2.**Hora (iSifundo saseNatal):**Uhlalutyo lwetshathi yokuzalwa komntu ngamnye. Ifunda indlela ulungelelwaniso lweplanethi ekuzalweni oluhambelana ngayo nomlingiswa womntu, amandla kunye nendlela yobomi.
3.**ISamhita (iSifundo soMdane):**Uphononongo lweziganeko ezidibeneyo, iipateni zendalo, kunye nemijikelo ebanzi yempucuko yabantu.

## IiNakshatras ezingama-27: Iindawo zokuhlala zaseLunar

IiNakshatra ziindlu ezinkulu ezingama-27 zeVédic Astrology—amakroza apho iNyanga ihamba khona kumjikelezo wayo wenyanga. Ukuzalwa kwakho i-Nakshatra yenye yezona zinto zibalulekileyo kwitshathi yakho, emele imeko yendalo yengqondo yakho:

*   **Ashwini:**Isantya, amandla okuphilisa, kunye neziqalo ezintsha.
*   **Bharani:**Inguqu enzulu kunye nokuhlaziywa.
*   **Krittika:**Ukucoca ukugxila, ukucaca, kunye nokuziphatha.
*   **Rohini:**Ukudala, ubuhle, kunye nokufudumala kweemvakalelo.
*   **UMrigashira:**Ukufuna ukwazi, ukukhangela, kunye nophononongo olubuthathaka.
*   **Ardra:**Ukucoca izaqhwithi kunye nokuhlaziywa kokuyila.
*   **Punarvasu:**Ukubuyiselwa, ukukhuselwa, kunye neziqalo ezintsha.
*   **Pushya:**Isondlo sokomoya, ubulumko, nenyameko.
*   **Ashlesha:**Imfihlelo, ubunzulu, kunye namandla angaphakathi agxile.

## I-Vimshottari Dasha: Imijikelezo yePlanethi

I-Vimshottari Dasha yeyona nkqubo ithembekileyo kakhulu yexesha leplanethi eJyotish. Ijonga umjikelo opheleleyo wobomi bomntu obunokubakho weminyaka eli-120 njengolandelelwano lwamaxesha eplanethi, ngalinye lilawulwa ngamandla asithoba eplanethi:

*   **Ketu (iminyaka eyi-7):**Ukucamngca ngokomoya, ukucinga ngaphakathi, kunye neengcambu zangaphambili.
*   **IVenus (iminyaka engama-20):**Imvisiswano yokudala, ubudlelwane, kunye nokucokiswa.
*   **Ilanga (iminyaka emi-6):**Amandla angaphakathi, ukugxila, injongo, kunye nokucaca.
*   **Inyanga (iminyaka eyi-10):**Ukondla ngokweemvakalelo, ukwamkeleka, kunye neengcambu zosapho.
*   **Mars (iminyaka eyi-7):**Amandla abalulekileyo, ingqalelo, inkalipho, kunye nesenzo.
*   **URahu (iminyaka eyi-18):**Amabhongo, i-horizons yangaphandle, kunye nezifundo zehlabathi.
*   **Jupiter (Iminyaka eyi-16):**Ubulumko, ukukhula ngokomoya, ukwanda, nokufundisisa.
*   **Saturn (Iminyaka eyi-19):**Ukuziphatha, uxanduva, umonde, kunye nokufunda okuqinileyo.
*   **IMercury (iminyaka eyi-17):**Ukucaca kwengqondo, urhwebo, kunye nonxibelelwano.
