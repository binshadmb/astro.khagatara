# Itshathi yokuzalwa yasimahla: iVédic Kundali yakho kwi-Intanethi

Itshathi yakho yokuzalwa yimephu yezulu kanye ngexesha lokuzalwa kwakho. Sifitshane sendalo iphela kanye kanye ngeyona ndlela ufika ngayo kulo mhlaba. I-Védic Astrology, eneminyaka engaphezu kwama-5,000 yembali, isebenzisa le tshati ukubonisa amandla akho angaphakathi, utyekelo lwendalo, kunye nemijikelo yecosmic ebumba amandla akho.

---

## Yintoni iTshati yokuZalwa yaseVédic?

Ngokungafaniyo ne-Western Astrology ethembele kakhulu kwiSibonakaliso seLanga, i-Védic Astrology igxile kwiSibonakaliso seNyanga-uphawu apho iNyanga ibekwe khona ekuzalweni kwakho. Lo mqondiso weNyanga, wabiza**URashi**kwiSanskrit, imele indalo yakho yeemvakalelo enzulu, ithuku lakho, kunye nengqondo yakho engaphantsi kwengqondo.

## Yintoni iNakshatra okanye iNkwenkwezi yokuzalwa?

INakshatra yinkwenkwezi yakho yokuzalwa-indlu enkulu yenyanga apho iNyanga yayihlala khona ekuzalweni kwakho. I-Védic Astrology yahlula iinkwenkwezi zibe ziiNakshatra ezingama-27, nganye kuzo ilawulwa ngamandla athile, isithixo esilawulayo nenjongo ethile. I-Nakshatra yakho ityhila isimilo sakho esinzulu, izipho zokomoya, kunye namandla athile owazilawulayo kobu bomi.

## Inkqubo yeDasha: Imijikelezo yexesha lePlanethi

Inkqubo yeDasha yenye yezona zixhobo zinzulu zeVédic Astrology. Yahlula ubomi bakho bube ngamaxesha amakhulu alawulwa ziiplanethi ezahlukeneyo, nganye izisa impembelelo yamandla eyahlukileyo. Ukuqonda iDasha yakho yangoku kukuvumela ukuba uqonde amandla eplanethi akhoyo achaphazela ingqondo yakho ngoku, ukuxhasa ukuqonda komntu kunye nengqondo.
