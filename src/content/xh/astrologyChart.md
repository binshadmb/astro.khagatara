# Itshathi yokuvumisa ngeenkwenkwezi yasimahla: Fumanisa ulungelelwaniso lwakho olusecaleni

Itshathi yakho yokuvumisa ngeenkwenkwezi ingaphezulu lee kunoluhlu olulula lweeplanethi nemiqondiso. Lulwimi lwezibalo lwamazulu olubhalwe kanye ngexesha lokuzalwa kwakho. I-Védic Astrology (Jyotish), ephuma kwi-Indiya yakudala, ibonelela ngeyona ndlela yehlabathi yokutolika ngokuchanekileyo nangokunzulu ngokomoya kwale mephu yecosmic.

---

## Yintoni iTshati yeVédic Astrology?

Itshathi yeVédic Astrology, ngokuqhelekileyo eyaziwa njenge**Kundali**okanye**itshathi yokuzalwa**, yimephu yejometri egqibeleleyo yeendawo zeplanethi ngexesha lakho lokuzalwa. Ngokungafaniyo neenkwenkwezi zaseNtshona, iJyotish isebenzisa i**I-zodiac esecaleni**—eyona ndawo ikuyo izijikelezi-langa namakroza esibhakabhakeni namhlanje—nto leyo eyenza ichane kakhulu.

---

## Iiplanethi ezili-9 zeVédic (Grahas)

E-Jyotish, imizimba yeplanethi elithoba imele imiba engundoqo yolwazi lwethu lwangaphakathi kunye namandla:

*   **Surya (Ngelanga):**Umphefumlo, ubunkokeli, injongo, kunye namandla angaphakathi.
*   **Chandra (iNyanga):**Ingqondo, iimvakalelo, ukukhuliswa komama, kunye ne-intuition.
*   **Mangala (Mars):**Ubunzima, ukuqhuba, isibindi, isenzo kunye namandla omzimba.
*   **UBhuda (iMercury):**Ubukrelekrele, unxibelelwano, ukucaca kohlalutyo, kunye norhwebo.
*   **Guru (Jupiter):**Ubulumko, ukukhula ngokomoya, ukwanda, ukufunda, kunye nobabalo.
*   **Shukra (Venus):**Uthando, imvisiswano yobugcisa, ubudlelwane, ukucokisa kunye nobuhle.
*   **UShani (Saturn):**Ukuziphatha, umonde, uxanduva, kunye nolwakhiwo.
*   **I-Rahu (iNdawo yoMntla):**Iminqweno yamazwi, izifundo eziphathekayo, ukusungula izinto ezintsha, kunye nokwandiswa.
*   **I-Ketu (iNdawo ekuMazantsi):**Ukukhululwa kwangaphakathi, ibutho lomoya, iingcambu ezidlulileyo, kunye nenkululeko.

## Sidereal vs. Tropical Zodiac Ukuchaneka

I-Western Astrology isebenzisa i**Iinkwenkwezi zeTropiki**, emiselwe ngamaxesha onyaka onyaka. I-Védic Astrology isebenzisa i**I-zodiac esecaleni**, nto leyo emiliselwe kwezona ndawo zimi kuzo iinkwenkwezi.

Ngenxa ye-precesión ye-equinoxes, ngoku kukho umahluko omalunga nama-23 degrees phakathi kwezi nkqubo zimbini. Yingakho i-Védic Moon Sign yakho okanye i-Ascendant ihlala ihluke kwisibonakaliso sakho saseNtshona-ukulungelelanisa ngokuthe ngqo kunye nenyaniso ebonakalayo yesibhakabhaka ebusuku.

---

## Ukuqonda imijikelo yakho yexesha elinamandla

Enye yezona zinto zibaluleke kakhulu kwitshathi yeVédic kukukwazi ukwenza imephu yemijikelo yamandla ngokuhamba kwexesha. Ukusebenzisa i-Vimshottari Dasha kunye nokuhamba kweplanethi, sinokuwaqonda amaxesha eeplanethi ngoku aphembelela iingcinga zakho, impilo, kunye nemimandla yobudlelwane, ukubonelela ngesibuko esihle sokuzazi kunye nokuphila okunengqondo.
