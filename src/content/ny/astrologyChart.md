# Tchati Chaulere cha Nyenyezi: Dziwani Mayendedwe Anu Ambali

Tchati chanu chokhulupirira nyenyezi ndi choposa mndandanda wamba wa mapulaneti ndi zizindikiro. Ndilo chinenero cha masamu chakumwamba cholembedwa pa nthawi yeniyeni imene munabadwa. Védic Astrology (Jyotish), yochokera ku India wakale, imapereka matanthauzidwe olondola kwambiri a zakuthambo komanso ozama mwauzimu a mapu a zakuthambo.

---

## Kodi Tchati cha Védic Astrology ndi chiyani?

Tchati cha Védic Astrology, chomwe chimadziwika kuti a**Kundali**kapena**tchati chobadwa**, ndi mapu abwino kwambiri a geometric a malo a mapulaneti panthawi yomwe munabadwa. Mosiyana ndi zaku Western, Jyotish amagwiritsa ntchito**mbali ya zodiac**—malo enieni a mapulaneti ndi magulu a nyenyezi akumwamba lerolino—kulipangitsa kukhala lolondola kwambiri.

---

## Mapulaneti 9 a Védic (Grahas)

Ku Jyotish, matupi a mapulaneti asanu ndi anayi amayimira mbali zazikulu za chidziwitso chathu chamkati ndi mphamvu:

*   **Surya (Dzuwa):**Moyo, utsogoleri, cholinga, ndi mphamvu zamkati.
*   **Chandra (Mwezi):**Malingaliro, malingaliro, chisamaliro cha amayi, ndi chidziwitso.
*   **Mangala (Mars):**Mphamvu, kuyendetsa, kulimba mtima, kuchitapo kanthu, ndi mphamvu zakuthupi.
*   **Buddha (Mercury):**Luntha, kulumikizana, kumveketsa bwino, ndi malonda.
*   **Guru (Jupiter):**Nzeru, kukula kwauzimu, kukula, kuphunzira, ndi chisomo.
*   **Shukra (Venus):**Chikondi, mgwirizano waluso, maubwenzi, kuwongolera, ndi kukongola.
*   **Shani (Saturn):**Chilango, kuleza mtima, udindo, ndi dongosolo.
*   **Rahu (North Node):**Zokhumba za mawu, maphunziro akuthupi, zatsopano, ndi kufalikira.
*   **Ketu (South Node):**Kumasulidwa kwamkati, kuchotsedwa kwauzimu, mizu yakale, ndi kumasulidwa.

---

## Sidereal vs. Tropical Zodiac Kulondola

Western Astrology imagwiritsa ntchito**Zodiac yotentha**, zomwe zimaikidwiratu ku nyengo zapachaka. Védic Astrology amagwiritsa ntchito**mbali ya zodiac**, zomwe zimakhazikika ku malo enieni a zakuthambo a nyenyezi.

Chifukwa cha kuyambika kwa ma equinoxes, pakali pano pali kusiyana pafupifupi madigiri 23 pakati pa machitidwe awiriwa. Ichi ndichifukwa chake Chizindikiro chanu cha Mwezi wa Védic kapena Ascendant nthawi zambiri chimakhala chosiyana ndi chizindikiro chanu cha Kumadzulo-kugwirizanitsa mwachindunji ndi zenizeni zenizeni za thambo la usiku.

## Kumvetsetsa Mayendedwe Anu Anthawi Yamphamvu

Chimodzi mwazinthu zofunika kwambiri pa tchati cha Védic ndi kuthekera kwake popanga mapu ozungulira mphamvu pakapita nthawi. Pogwiritsa ntchito Vimshottari Dasha ndi maulendo a mapulaneti, tikhoza kumvetsetsa nthawi za mapulaneti zomwe zikukhudza malingaliro anu, thanzi lanu, ndi maubwenzi anu, kukupatsani galasi lokongola kuti muzitha kudzidziwitsa nokha komanso kukhala ndi moyo woganizira.

## Un Espejo de tu Alma: The Personalized Cosmic Blueprint

Ili si lipoti lachisawawa lopangidwa ndi mapulogalamu amtundu uliwonse. Ndi mapu atsatanetsatane, owona mtima a zakuthambo omwe amawerengedwa mwatsatanetsatane masamu pogwiritsa ntchito maulendo enieni a mwezi wa zakuthambo komanso njira za makolo a Védic ndi Chaldean. Cholinga chathu chokha ndikukupatsani inu momveka bwino, ulemu, ndi chida chenicheni chodzizindikiritsa nokha ndi kulingalira.

### Kodi mukuyembekezera chiyani mkati mwa mapulani anu amasamba 8 (PDF)?

*   **Siginecha Yanu Yogwedezeka:**Kusanthula mozama kwa Nambala Yanu ya Moyo Wanu, Nambala Yanu Ya Dzina (Kachitidwe ka Chaldean), ndi kuyimba mwakachetechete kwa Moyo Wanu Urge.
*   **Kugwirizana kwa Ukwati ndi Mgwirizano wa Ubale:**Kumvetsetsa momwe ubale wanu umakhalira, momwe tchati chanu chakubadwa chimakokera mphamvu zenizeni, ndi makiyi a masamu achikhalidwe kuti mumange maubwenzi ozama, okhalitsa a m'banja potengera kumvana.
*   **Mphamvu Zanu Zothandizira:**Kuzindikira kwachikhalidwe pamitundu, kumveka kwa miyala yamtengo wapatali, ndi masiku a kuyanjanitsa komwe kumalimbikitsa kuyang'ana, mgwirizano, ndi positivity m'moyo wanu watsiku ndi tsiku.
*   **Kumvetsetsa Nthawi Yanu Yozungulira (Vimshottari Dasha):**Kuwona nthawi yapadziko lapansi yomwe mukukumana nayo pano, ikugwira ntchito ngati chimango chakale chodziwonetsera nokha, kuleza mtima, komanso nthawi yamkati.
*   **Chidziwitso cha Kulingalira:**Lipotili ndi kafukufuku wa masamu, opangidwa kuti azithandizira kudziganizira komanso kukula kwaumwini.
