## Nambala Zitatu Zazina Zazikulu

Tchati cha dzina lanu chili ndi manambala atatu ofunikira:

1.**Nambala Yofotokozera (Nambala ya Dzina):**Kuwerengeka pogwiritsa ntchito zilembo zonse za dzina lanu lonse lobadwa. Zimawulula maluso anu achilengedwe, mphamvu, ndi momwe mumafotokozera cholinga cha moyo wanu kudziko lapansi.
2.**Soul Urge Number (Chilakolako cha Mtima):**Kuwerengeka pogwiritsa ntchito mavawelo a dzina lanu okha. Imawulula zokhumba zanu zamkati, zolakalaka zamseri, ndi zomwe zimakhutitsa moyo wanu kuchokera mkati.
3.**Nambala Yamunthu:**Kuwerengera pogwiritsa ntchito makonsonanti a dzina lanu okha. Imavumbula mmene ena amakuonerani, chithunzithunzi chimene mumapanga, ndi umunthu wakunja umene mumapereka.
