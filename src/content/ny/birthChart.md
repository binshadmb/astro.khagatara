## Kodi Tchati cha Kubadwa kwa Védic ndi chiyani?

Mosiyana ndi Western Astrology yomwe imadalira kwambiri Chizindikiro cha Dzuwa, Védic Astrology imayang'ana pa Chizindikiro cha Mwezi-chizindikiro chomwe Mwezi udayikidwa pakubadwa kwanu. Chizindikiro cha Mwezi ichi, chotchedwa**Rashi**mu Sanskrit, imayimira umunthu wanu wakuzama, chibadwa chanu, ndi malingaliro anu osazindikira.

## Kodi Nakshatra kapena Birth Star ndi chiyani?

Nakshatra ndiye nyenyezi yanu yobadwira - nyumba yayikulu yomwe Mwezi umakhala pakubadwa kwanu. Védic Astrology imagawa zodiac kukhala Nakshatras 27, iliyonse yolamulidwa ndi mphamvu inayake, mulungu wolamulira, ndi cholinga. Nakshatra wanu amawulula umunthu wanu wakuya, mphatso zauzimu, ndi mphamvu zenizeni zomwe mudadziwa m'moyo uno.
