## Dɛn ne jyotish?

jyotish yɛ Sanskrit asɛmfua a ɛkyerɛ "hann ho nyansahu" anaa "ɔsoro nneɛma ho nyansahu." Ɛyɛ Vedanga asia no mu biako—Veda, tete nhoma kronkron no nteɛso aboafo. Rishifo—tete India adehufo anyansafo—a wɔhwɛɛ amansan no akontaabu nhyehyɛe a edi mũ na wohuu abusuabɔ a ɛda wɔne nnipa adwene ntam no na wɔyɛɛ jyotish.

Wɔakyekyɛ jyotish nyansahu kɛse no mu ayɛ no adum atitiriw abiɛsa:

3. 3. .**Samhita (Nnanase Adesua): .**Nsɛm a esisi a wɔaboaboa ano, abɔde mu nneɛma a ɛkɔ so, ne nnipa anibuei ahorow a ɛtrɛw no ho adesua.

## vimshottari dasha: Okyinnsoromma ahorow no kyinhyia

vimshottari dasha ne okyinnsoromma mmere nhyehyɛe a wogye di sen biara wɔ jyotish. Ebu onipa nkwa nna a ebetumi ayɛ mfe 120 a edi mũ sɛ okyinnsoromma ahorow so mmere a ɛtoatoa so, a okyinnsoromma ahoɔden akron no mu biako na edi emu biara so:

*   **Ketu (Mfeɛ 7): .**Honhom mu nsusuwii, emu nsusuwii, ne ntini a atwam.
*   **Venus (Mfe 20): .**Adebɔ mu biakoyɛ, abusuabɔ, ne ahotew.
*   **Owia (Mfe 6): .**Ahoɔden a ɛwɔ mu, adwene a wɔde si biribi so, atirimpɔw, ne nea emu da hɔ.
*   **Ɔsram (Mfe 10): .**Nkate mu ntetee, gye a wogye tom, ne abusua ntini.
*   **Mars (Mfe 7): .**Ahoɔden a ɛho hia, adwene a wɔde si biribi so, akokoduru, ne adeyɛ.
*   **Rahu (Mfeɛ 18): .**Apɛde, amannɔne nsusuwii, ne wiase asuade ahorow.
*   **Jupiter (Mfe 16): Ɔde ne nsa kyerɛɛ ne so.**Nyansa, honhom mu nkɔso, ntrɛwmu, ne adesua.
*   **Saturn (Mfeɛ 19): .**Nteɛso, asɛyɛde, boasetɔ, ne adesua a ɛyɛ den.
*   **Mercury (Mfeɛ 17): .**Adwene mu pefeeyɛ, aguadi, ne nkitahodi.
