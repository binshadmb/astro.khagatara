## He aha te Tutohi Whanautanga Védic?

Kaore i rite ki te Western astrology e whakawhirinaki nui ana ki te Ra Waitohu, ka aro a Védic astrology ki te Moon Sign—te tohu i tu ai te Marama i to whanautanga. ## He aha te nakshatra, Whanau Whetu ranei?

Ko te nakshatra to whetu whanau—te whare marama i noho ai te Marama i to whanautanga. Ka wehewehea e Védic astrology te zodiac ki te 27 nakshatras, e whakahaeretia ana e te kaha motuhake, te atua rangatira, me te kaupapa.
