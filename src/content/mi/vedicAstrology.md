## Ko nga Peka e toru a jyotish

Kua wehea te maatauranga nui o jyotish ki nga pou matua e toru:

1.**Ganita (Astronomy and Calculation):**Te turanga pangarau. Ka tatauhia e ia nga tuunga tika o nga whetu me nga aorangi ma te whakamahi i te tohu zodiac taha—te tuunga tinana o nga kahui whetu i te rangi i enei ra.
2.**Hora (Natal Study):**Te tātaritanga o te tūtohi whanau takitahi. Ka rangahauhia he pehea te hangai o te aorangi i te whanautanga ki te ahua o te tangata, te kaha, me te huarahi oranga.
3.**Samhita (Matauranga o te ao):**Ko te rangahau o nga huihuinga huinga, nga tauira taiao, me nga huringa whanui o nga ao tangata.

## The Vimshottari Dasha: Planetary Cycles

The Vimshottari Dasha is the most widely trusted system of planetary periods in Jyotish. It views a complete potential human life cycle of 120 years as a sequence of planetary periods, each governed by one of the nine planetary energies:

*   **Ketu (7 Years):** Spiritual contemplation, inner reflection, and past roots.
*   **Venus (20 Years):** Creative harmony, relationships, and refinement.
*   **Sun (6 Years):** Inner strength, focus, purpose, and clarity.
*   **Moon (10 Years):** Emotional nurturing, receptivity, and family roots.
*   **Mars (7 Years):** Vital energy, focus, courage, and action.
*   **Rahu (18 Years):** Ambition, foreign horizons, and worldly lessons.
*   **Jupiter (16 Years):** Wisdom, spiritual growth, expansion, and study.
*   **Saturn (19 Years):** Discipline, responsibility, patience, and solid learning.
*   **Mercury (17 Years):** Intellectual clarity, commerce, and communication.
