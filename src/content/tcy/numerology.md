## What is a Life Path Number?

Your Life Path Number is the most important number in your numerology chart. It is derived from the exact numbers of your birthdate. This number acts as a spiritual mirror, revealing your core personality traits, your natural talents, and the specific spiritual lessons you chose to explore in this lifetime.

## How to Calculate Your Life Path Number

To calculate your Life Path Number, you sum all the digits of your birthdate (day, month, and year) and reduce them to a single digit between 1 and 9.

However, if your sum equals **11, 22, or 33**, do not reduce them. These are called **Master Numbers** and represent highly elevated vibrations and deeper self-reflection.

### Step-by-Step Example
If you were born on June 13, 1981:
1. **Reduce the Day:** 13 → 1 + 3 = 4
2. **Reduce the Month:** June is the 6th month → 6
3. **Reduce the Year:** 1981 → 1 + 9 + 8 + 1 = 19 → 1 + 9 = 10 → 1 + 0 = 1
4. **Sum the Totals:** 4 (Day) + 6 (Month) + 1 (Year) = 11
*Result: **Life Path 11** — The Master Intuitive.*

## Védic vs. Western Numerology

While Western Numerology (Pythagorean) is highly popular, **Védic Numerology** (originating in ancient India) integrates numbers directly with astronomical moon transits.

Rather than looking at numbers in isolation, the Védic approach combines your birth date with your Moon Sign (**Rashi**), Birth Star (**Nakshatra**), and current planetary cycle (**Dasha**). This offers a significantly more complete, deep, and harmonious view of your energetic alignment.
