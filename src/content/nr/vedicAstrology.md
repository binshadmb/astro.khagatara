# I-Védic astrology yamahhala: Ukukhanya kwe-jyotish ku-inthanethi

I-jyotish—i-Védic astrology—yaziwa ngokwesiko ngokuthi "yisayensi yokukhanya." Ngeminyaka engaphezu kwe-5,000 yomlando ongapheliko, ngelinye lamahlelo ahlakaniphileko wokuzizwisisa athuthukiswe babantu. Kunokobana achaze ubuntu obujayelekileko, u-jyotish uhlola amaphetheni we-cosmic atjhingako kanye nemijikelezo yesikhathi esiza ukuletha ukuzwana epilwenakho.

---

## Khuyini u-jyotish?

I-jyotish ligama lesi-Sanskrit elitjho "isayensi yokukhanya" nofana "isayensi yezinto ezisezulwini." Ngenye yama-Vedanga asithandathu—iimfundo ezisizako zama-Vedas, imitlolo engcwele yakade. I-jyotish yasungulwa ma-Rishis—ababoni abahlakaniphileko be-India yakade—abaqala amaphetheni weembalo apheleleko we-cosmos begodu babona ukuhlobana kwawo nokuqaphela kwabantu.

---

## Amagatja amathathu we-jyotish

Isayensi ekulu ye-jyotish ihlukaniswe yaba ziinsika ezintathu eziqakathekileko:

1.**U-Ganita (I-Astronomy kanye ne-Calculation):**Isisekelo seembalo. Ibala iindawo eziqondileko zeenkwekwezi namaplanethi ngokusebenzisa i-sidereal zodiac—indawo yamambala yamaqoqo weenkwekwezi esibhakabhakeni namhlanjesi.
2.**I-Hora (i-Natal Study):**Ukuhlaziywa kwetjhadi lokubelethwa komuntu ngamunye. Ifunda ngokuthi ukulungiswa kwamaplanethi ekubelethweni kuhambelana njani nobuntu bomuntu, amandla, nendlela yokuphila.
3.**I-Samhita (Ifundo ye-Mundane):**Isifundo sezehlakalo ezihlangeneko, amaphetheni wemvelo, kanye nemijikelezo ebanzi yempucuko yabantu.

---

## Ama-27 nakshatras: Ama-Lunar Mansions

I-nakshatras ziindlu ezima-27 zenyanga ze-Védic astrology—amaqoqo weenkwekwezi lapho iNyanga ikhamba khona emjikelezweni wayo wenyanga. I-nakshatra yakho yokubelethwa ngenye yezinto eziqakatheke khulu etjhadini lakho, ejamele ubujamo bemvelo bengqondo yakho:

*   **Ashwini:**Ijubane, amandla wokuphulukisa, kanye nokuthoma okutjha.
*   **Bharani:**Ukutjhuguluka okujulileko nokuvuselelwa.
*   **Krittika:**Ukuhlanza ukutjheja, ukukhanya, nokuziphatha.
*   **U-Rohini:**Ubuciko, ubuhle, kanye nokufuthumala kwemizwa.
*   **Mrigashira:**Ukufuna ukwazi, ukufuna, nokuhlola okubucayi.
*   **Ardra:**Ukuhlanza iinwuruwuru nokuvuselelwa kokudala.
*   **Punarvasu:**Ukubuyiselwa, ukuvikelwa, nokuthoma okutjha.
*   **Pushya:**Ukondleka ngokomoya, ukuhlakanipha, nokutlhogomela.
*   **Ashlesha:**Imfihlakalo, ukujula, namandla wangaphakathi agxile.
*   *(Begodu namanye ama-nakshatras amahle ali-18, enye nenye ithwele amandla wayo wokuthuthumela)*

## I-vimshottari dasha: Imijikelezo yamaplanethi

I-vimshottari dasha lihlelo elithenjwa khulu leenkhathi zamaplanethi e-jyotish. Iqala umjikelezo wokuphila womuntu opheleleko weminyaka eli-120 njengokulandelana kweenkhathi zamaplanethi, enye nenye ilawulwa ngenye yamandla weplanethi alithoba:

*   **I-Ketu (Iminyaka eli-7):**Ukucabangisisa ngokomoya, ukuzindla kwangaphakathi, kanye nemisuka edlulileko.
*   **I-Venus (Iminyaka ema-20):**Ukuzwana kokutlama, ubudlelwano, nokulungiswa.
*   **Ilanga (Iminyaka eyi-6):**Amandla wangaphakathi, ukutjheja, umnqopho, nokukhanya.
*   **Inyanga (Iminyaka eli-10):**Ukukhuliswa ngokomzwelo, ukwamukela, kanye nemirabhu yomndeni.
*   **I-Mars (Iminyaka eli-7):**Amandla aqakathekileko, ukutjheja, isibindi, nesenzo.
*   **U-Rahu (Iminyaka eli-18):**Ukuzikhakhazisa, ubujamo bangaphandle, kanye neemfundo zephasi.
*   **I-Jupiter (Iminyaka eli-16):**Ukuhlakanipha, ukukhula ngokomoya, ukwanda, nokufunda.
*   **I-Saturn (Iminyaka eli-19):**Ukuziphatha, ukuziphendulela, ukubekezela, nokufunda okuqinileko.
*   **I-Mercury (Iminyaka eli-17):**Ukukhanya kwengqondo, ukurhweba, nokukhulumisana.

## I-Un Espejo de tu Alma: Ipulani ye-Cosmic eyenziwe ngomuntu mathupha

Lokhu akusiwo umbiko ongahlelekile owenziwe yisofthiwe ejwayelekileko. Yimephu ye-cosmic eneenkcukacha, eqotho ebalwe ngokunembileko kweembalo kusetjenziswa ukudlula kwenyanga kwamambala kweenkwekwezi kanye namafomula wama-Védic nama-chaldean. Umnqopho wethu kukunikela ngokukhanyako, ihlonipho, kanye nethulusi lamambala lokuzithola nokucabanga.

### Khuyini elindele ngaphakathi kwehlelo lakho lamakhasi ayi-8 (i-PDF)?

*   **Isiginetjha yakho yokudlidliza:**Ukuhlaziywa okujulileko kweNomboro yakho yeNdlela yePilo, Inomboro yakho yeGama (ihlelo lamaKhaledi), nokubiza okuthulileko kwe-Soul Urge yakho.
*   **Ukulungiswa komtjhado nokuvumelana kobudlelwano:**Ukuzwisisa amaphetheni wobudlelwano bakho, bona itjhadi lakho lokubelethwa lidosa njani amandla athileko, kanye namakhiye weembalo wesintu wokwakha ubuhlobo obujulileko, obuhlala isikhathi eside bomtjhado obusekelwe ekuhambisaneni.
*   **Amandla wakho asekelako:**Ukuzwisisa kwendabuko ngemibala, ukuzwakala kwamatje aligugu, kanye namalanga wokulinganisa akhuthaza ukutjheja, ukuzwana, nokuhle epilwenakho yangelanga.
*   **Ukuzwisisa umjikelezo wesikhathi sakho samanje (vimshottari dasha):**Ukuhlola isikhathi seplanethi esijayelekileko ohlangabezana naso njenganje, okusebenza njengehlaka lakade lokuzindla, ukubekezela, kanye nesikhathi sangaphakathi.
*   **Inothi lokucabangisisa:**Umbiko lo ufundwa ngamaphetheni weembalo, owenzelwe ukusekela ukuzicabangisisa nokukhula komuntu mathupha.
