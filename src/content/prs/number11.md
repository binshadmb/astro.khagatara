## What is a Master Number?

Master Numbers (11, 22, and 33) carry a double digit of identical numbers, representing a highly concentrated double vibration. 11 carries a double vibration of 1, 22 carries a double vibration of 2, and 33 carries a double vibration of 3. This dual resonance represents a heightened potential for awareness, accompanied by specific life lessons that encourage deep personal discipline.
