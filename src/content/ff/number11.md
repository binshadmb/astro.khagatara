# Maanaa Limngal 11: Limngal Jaagorgal Limngal

Limre 11 ina jeyaa e Limooje tati mawɗe e nder limlebbi—e sara 22 e 33. E nder njuɓɓudiiji gaadanteeji, nde meeɗaa ustaade haa limre wootere sabu nde jogii ko dimmbagol toowngol ruuhuyankeewol e teskinngol. Yimɓe jogiiɓe Limoore Laawol Nguurndam 11 ina keewi limteede ko njiylawu luggiɗngu jogiingu semmbe keeriiɗo no feewi e nder nguurndam ɗam.

---

## Hol ko woni Limoore Jaagorgal?

Limɗe mawɗe (11, 22, e 33) ina njogii diidi ɗiɗi limle nannduɗe, kollirooje dimmbagol ɗiɗi tiiɗngol no feewi. 11 ina jogii diƴƴe ɗiɗi 1, 22 ina jogii diƴƴe ɗiɗi 2, 33 ina jogii diƴƴe ɗiɗi 3. Ngolɗoo diƴƴe ɗiɗi ina hollita mbaawka ɓeydaaka ngam anndude, tawa ina yahdi e janngirɗe nguurndam keertiiɗe, ɗe cemmbinta nehdi neɗɗo luggiɗndi.

---

## Sifaaji mawɗi limngal Master 11

Limre 11 ndee ina seerti e sifaaji keewɗi keertiiɗi :

*   **Intuition ɓeydaande:**Heɓde miijooji laaɓtuɗi e miijooji guurɗi laaɓtuɗi no feewi. Daande maɓɓe nder ko kompas maɓɓe ɓurɗo mawnude.
*   **Sensitibilite luggiɗnde:**Baawɗe yurmeende ngam yiyde semmbeeji e miijooji taariiɗi ɗii haa laaɓi.
*   **Kalite cemmbinɗo:**Baawɗe nafooje ngam ustude, ɓamtude, e renndinde yiɗde e woɗɓe.
*   **Dualism nder:**Balance hakkunde drive active (1) e muñal jaɓɓoowo (2), sosde moter belɗo ngam mawnugol nder.
*   **Faandaare toownde:**Diɗɗal deƴƴungal ngam wuurde e faandaare, yiɗde jokkondire e anndinde hoore mum e kala golle.
*   **Sensitibilite semmbe:**Semmbe nervous e hakkille golloowo no feewi, ɗaɓɓoowo hakkille e hakkille.

## Limre 11 e nder Jokkondiral e Nanondiral

E nder jokkondiral, jogiiɓe limoore 11 ina njiɗi jokkondiral goongawal, luggiɗngal, laaɓtungal. Ɓe mbeltiima no feewi so tawii jom suudu maɓɓe ina hormii sensitivity maɓɓe toowɗo e yiɗde maɓɓe keeriinde e nguurndam. Devoted e luggiɗde e toppitaade, ɓe ƴellitoo e bannge gollodiiɓe rokkooɓe deeƴre e grounding emotional. Ko ɓuri heewde e harmonie limoore maɓɓe ina heewi taweede e limooje 2, 6, e limooje Master goɗɗe.

---

## Limre 11 e nder Focus & Alignment ñalnde kala

Yimɓe jogiiɓe limoore 11 ina mbeltii e nokkuuji baawɗi huutoraade hakkillaaji mum en e yiytere mum en teskinnde. Ɓe ɓuri waawde ko e fannuuji jowitiiɗi e naalankaagal, jimɗi, binndol, ardorde neɗɗo, hakkille, e wasiyaaji. Ɓe cemmbinaama no feewi e mahde golle ñalnde kala ballitooje hakkille e deeƴre hakkille, rewrude e golle mekanik ɗe njuumtata no feewi, ɗe njahdaani e doole maɓɓe ndernderiije.

## Njiylawu aadaaji e limooje mawɗe

Limɗe tati mawɗe ɗee ina kolla keewal ruuhu ceertungal:

*   **11 (Yiyoowo):**Focus dow ƴoƴre, iwdi, e anndal ruuhu luggiɗngal.
*   **22 (Mawɗo mahoowo):**Fotde yiyngo gollorngo, mahngo tuugnorgal duumotoongal, e feeñninde miijooji.
*   **33 (Jannginoowo oo):**Fotde ballal winndere, gollal yurmeende, e ƴellitde hakkille.
