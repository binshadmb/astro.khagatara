Numerology can reveal a great deal about the energetic compatibility between two individuals. ## How Numerology Compatibility Works

Numerology compatibility is primarily based on comparing the Life Path Numbers of two people. However, a complete study also evaluates Name Numbers, Soul Urges, and Personality Numbers. When these numbers align harmoniously, relationship dynamics tend to flow with natural ease. When there is numerical tension, it represents areas where greater patience, understanding, and mutual respect are needed.
