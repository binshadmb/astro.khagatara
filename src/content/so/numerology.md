# Akhriska Tirooyinka Bilaashka ah: Soo hel Sawir-gacmeedkaaga Cosmic

Numerology waa mid ka mid ah nidaamyada is-helidda ugu qadiimsan adduunka. Kumanaan sano, dhaqamada adduunka oo dhan waxay isticmaaleen tirooyin si ay u fahmaan shakhsiyadda, cilaaqaadka, iyo socodka dabiiciga ah ee nolosha. Maanta, waxaad ku xisaabin kartaa dariiqaaga nolosha iyo tirooyinka cosmic si buuxda oo bilaash ah nidaamkayaga isku dhafan ee Kaldayiin iyo Védic numerology.

---

## Waa maxay Lambarka Jidka Nolosha?

Nambarkaaga Jidka Nolosha waa lambarka ugu muhiimsan shaxda lambarkaaga. Waxa laga soo qaatay tirooyinka saxda ah ee taariikhda dhalashadaada. Tiradani waxay u shaqaysaa sidii muraayad ruuxi ah, oo muujinaysa dabeecadahaaga shakhsi ahaaneed, kartidaada dabiiciga ah, iyo casharrada ruuxiga ah ee gaarka ah ee aad dooratay inaad sahamiso inta aad nooshahay.

---

## Sida Loo Xisaabiyo Lambarka Waddadaada Nolosha

Si aad u xisaabiso Nambarkaaga Jidka Nolosha, waxaad soo koobtaa dhammaan lambarrada taariikhda dhalashadaada (maalinta, bisha, iyo sannadka) oo ka dhig hal lambar oo u dhexeeya 1 iyo 9.

Si kastaba ha ahaatee, haddii wadartaadu le'eg tahay**11, 22, ama 33**, ha dhimin iyaga. Kuwaas waxaa lagu magacaabaa**Tirooyinka Masterka**waxayna matalaan gariir aad u sarreeya iyo is-milicsi qoto dheer.

### Tusaale Talaabo-Tallaabo ah
Haddii aad dhalatay Juun 13, 1981:
1.**Yaree Maalinta:**13 → 1 + 3 = 4
2.**Yaree Bisha:**Juun waa bisha 6aad → 6
3.**Yaree sanadka:**1981 → 1 + 9 + 8 + 1 = 19 → 1 + 9 = 10 → 1 + 0 = 1
4.**Isku soo wada duuboo:**4 (Maalin) + 6 (Bishii) + 1 (Sannad) = 11
*Natiijada:**Jidka Nolosha 11**- Dareenka Masterka.*

## Védic vs. Numerology-ga Galbeedka

Halka Western Numerology (Pythagorean) ay aad caan u tahay,**Védic Numerology**(oo asal ahaan ka soo jeeda Hindiya qadiimiga ah) waxay tirooyinka si toos ah ula midaysaa socodka dayax-gacmeedka.

Halkii aad ka eegi lahayd tirooyinka si gooni ah, habka Védic wuxuu isku daraa taariikhda dhalashadaada iyo calaamadda Dayaxa**Rashi**), Xiddiga Dhalashada (**Nakshatra**), iyo meertada meertada hadda jirta (**Dhasha**). Tani waxay ku siinaysaa aragti aad u dhammaystiran, qoto dheer, oo is waafaqsan oo ku saabsan toosinta tamartaada.
