Xexlẽdzesiwo ŋuti nunya nye xexeame ƒe blematɔ kekeake ƒe ɖoɖo siwo dzi woato ake ɖe wo ɖokui ŋu la dometɔ ɖeka. Ƒe akpe geɖe enye sia la, dekɔnuwo le xexeame godoo zãa xexlẽdzesiwo tsɔ sea amenyenye, ƒomedodowo, kple alesi agbe ƒe sisi le dzɔdzɔme nu gɔme. ## Nukae nye Agbemɔ ƒe Xexlẽdzesi?

Wò Agbemɔ Xexlẽdzesie nye xexlẽdzesi vevitɔ kekeake le wò xexlẽdzesiwo ƒe nɔnɔmetata me. Woɖee tso wò dzigbezã ƒe xexlẽdzesiwo tututu me. Xexlẽdzesi sia wɔa dɔ abe gbɔgbɔ me ahuhɔ̃e ene, si ɖea wò amenyenye ƒe nɔnɔme veviwo, wò dzɔdzɔmeŋutetewo, kple gbɔgbɔmenusɔsrɔ̃ tɔxɛ siwo nètia be yeaku le agbemeŋkeke sia me la fiana.

## Védic kple Ɣetoɖoƒetɔwo ƒe Xexlẽdzesiwo Ŋuti Nunya

Esia naa wò ŋusẽ ƒe ɖoɖowɔwɔ ƒe nukpɔsusu si de blibo wu, si de to wu, eye wòwɔ ɖeka wu ɖedzesi aɖe.
