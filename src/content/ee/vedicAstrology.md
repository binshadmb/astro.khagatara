# Free Védic Ɣletivimefakaka: jyotish ƒe Kekeli le Internet dzi

Woyɔa jyotish—Védic Ɣletivimefakaka—tso blema ke be “kekeli ŋuti dzɔdzɔmeŋutinunya.” Esi wònye ƒe 5,000 kple edzivɔ ƒe ŋutinya si megbã o ta la, enye ɖokuigɔmesese ƒe ɖoɖo deŋgɔtɔ kekeake siwo ameƒomea to vɛ la dometɔ ɖeka. Le esi teƒe be jyotish naɖɔ amenyenye ƒe nɔnɔme siwo bɔ ko la, edzroa xexeame ƒe nɔnɔme goglowo kple ɣeyiɣi ƒe tsatsam siwo kpena ɖe ŋuwò be ɖekawɔwɔ nanɔ wò agbe me la me.

---

## Nukae nye jyotish?

jyotish nye Sanskritgbe me nya si gɔmee nye "kekeli ŋuti dzɔdzɔmeŋutinunya" alo "dziƒonuwo ŋuti dzɔdzɔmeŋutinunya." Enye Vedanga adeawo dometɔ ɖeka—si nye Veda, siwo nye blemagbalẽ kɔkɔeawo, ƒe kpeɖeŋutɔ tohehewo. Rishitɔwo—blema India ƒe nukpɔla nunyalawo—siwo lé ŋku ɖe xexeame katã ƒe akɔntabubu ƒe ɖoɖo deblibowo ŋu eye wokpɔ woƒe kadodo kple amegbetɔ ƒe sidzedze dze sii lae to jyotish vɛ.

## jyotish ƒe Alɔdze Etɔ̃awo

Woma jyotish ƒe dzɔdzɔmeŋutinunya gã la ɖe sɔti vevi etɔ̃ me:

1. 1. .**Ganita (Ɣletiviŋutinunya Kple Akɔntabubu):**Akɔntabubu ƒe gɔmeɖoanyia. Ezãa ɣletivimefakaka si woyɔna be sidereal zodiac—si nye ɣletivihatsotso siwo le yame egbea ƒe teƒe ŋutɔŋutɔwo, tsɔ bua teƒe siwo tututu ɣletiviwo kple ɣletinyigbawo le.
2. 2. .**Hora (Vidzidzi Ŋuti Nusɔsrɔ̃):**Ame ɖekaɖekawo ƒe vidzidzi ƒe nɔnɔmetata me dzodzro. Esrɔ̃a nu tso alesi ɣletinyigbawo ƒe sɔsɔme le wo dzidzi me sɔ kple ame ɖekaɖeka ƒe nɔnɔme, ŋusẽ, kple agbenɔnɔmɔ ŋu.
3. 3. .**Samhita (Nusɔsrɔ̃ le Xexeame Katã ƒe Nusɔsrɔ̃):**Nusɔsrɔ̃ tso nudzɔdzɔ siwo dzɔna le ƒuƒoƒo me, dzɔdzɔmenuwo ƒe nɔnɔmewo, kple amegbetɔwo ƒe dekɔnuwo ƒe tsatsam gbadzaa ŋu.

## nakshatra 27-awo: Ɣletidzixɔwo

nakshatras nye Védic Ɣletivimefakaka ƒe dzinuxɔ 27—siwo nye ɣletivihatsotso siwo me Ɣletia tona le eƒe ɣleti sia ɣleti ƒe ɣletivimefakaka me. Wò dzidzi nakshatra nye nu vevi siwo le wò nɔnɔmetata me la dometɔ ɖeka, si tsi tre ɖi na wò susu ƒe dzɔdzɔmenɔnɔme:

*   **Ashwini:**Duƒuƒu, dɔyɔŋusẽ, kple gɔmedzedze yeyewo.
*   **Bharani:**Tɔtrɔ deto kple yeyewɔwɔ.
*   **Krittika:**Susu ƒe susu, eme kɔ, kple amehehe ŋuti kɔklɔ.
*   **Rohini ƒe nya:**Nuwɔwɔ, tugbedzedze, kple seselelãme ƒe vividodoɖeameŋu.
*   **Mrigashira:**Didi be yeanya nu, didi, kple numekuku si me susu le.
*   **Ardra:**Ahomwo ŋu kɔklɔ kple nutovɛwo ƒe yeyewɔwɔ.
*   **Punarvasu ƒe ŋkɔ:**Gbugbɔgaɖoanyi, ametakpɔkpɔ, kple gɔmedzedze yeyewo.
*   **Pushya:**Gbɔgbɔ me nunyiame, nunya, kple belélename.
*   **Ashlesha:**Nya ɣaɣla, goglome, kple ŋusẽ ememetɔ si ŋu wotsia dzi ɖo.
*   *(Eye nakshatra dzeani 18 bubu, siwo dometɔ ɖesiaɖe tsɔa eya ŋutɔ ƒe ʋuʋudedi ƒe ŋusẽ tɔxɛ)*

## vimshottari dasha: Ɣletinyigbawo ƒe Tsitrenɔnɔ

vimshottari dasha ye nye ɣletinyigbawo ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo ƒe ɖoɖo si dzi woka ɖo wu le jyotish. Ebua amegbetɔ ƒe agbenɔnɔ ƒe tsatsam blibo si ate ŋu anye ƒe 120 be enye ɣletinyigbawo ƒe ɣeyiɣi siwo kplɔ wo nɔewo ɖo, eye ɣletinyigba ƒe ŋusẽ enyiawo dometɔ ɖeka kpɔa wo dometɔ ɖesiaɖe dzi:

*   **Ketu (Ƒe 7):**Gbɔgbɔ me ŋugbledede, ememe ŋugbledede, kple ke siwo va yi.
*   **Venus (Ƒe 20):**Nuwɔwɔ ƒe ɖekawɔwɔ, ƒomedodowo, kple asitɔtrɔ le wo ŋu.
*   **Ɣe (Ƒe 6):**Ŋusẽ ememetɔ, susu tsɔtsɔ ɖo nu ŋu, taɖodzinu, kple eme kɔ nyuie.
*   **Ɣleti (Ƒe 10):**Seselelãme ƒe vihehe, xɔxɔ, kple ƒome ƒe kewo.
*   **Mars (Ƒe 7):**Ŋusẽ vevi, susu tsɔtsɔ ɖo nu ŋu, dzideƒo, kple nuwɔna.
*   **Rahu (Ƒe 18):**Didi vevie be yeaxɔ ŋkɔ, duta nukpɔsusuwo, kple xexeame ƒe nusɔsrɔ̃wo.
*   **Jupiter (Ƒe 16): .**Nunya, gbɔgbɔ me tsitsi, kekeɖenudɔ, kple nusɔsrɔ̃.
*   **Saturn (Ƒe 19):**Tohehe, agbanɔamedzi, dzigbɔɖi, kple nusɔsrɔ̃ sesẽ.
*   **Mercury (Ƒe 17): .**Nunya me kɔ, asitsatsa, kple kadodo.

## Un Espejo de tu Alma: Xexeame ƒe Nɔnɔmetata si Wowɔ na Ame ŋutɔ

Esia menye nyatakaka si wowɔ le vome si kɔmpiutadziɖoɖo siwo wozãna le mɔ vovovowo nu wɔ o. Enye xexeame ƒe anyigbatata si me kɔ, si tso dzime, si wobu akɔnta le akɔntabubu me pɛpɛpɛ to ɣletiviŋutinunya ƒe dzinu ƒe mɔzɔzɔ ŋutɔŋutɔwo kple tɔgbui Védic kple Kaldeatɔwo ƒe mɔfiamewo zazã me. Míaƒe taɖodzinu ɖeka koe nye be míana eme nakɔ, bubu, kple dɔwɔnu vavãtɔ si ana nàke ɖe ɖokuiwò ŋu ahatsɔ susu aɖo nu ŋu.
