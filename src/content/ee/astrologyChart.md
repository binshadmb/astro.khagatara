# Ɣletivimefakaka ƒe Tabla Femaxee: Ke Ðe Wò Sidereal Alignment Ŋu

Wò ɣletivimefakaka ƒe nɔnɔmetata la de ŋgɔ sã wu ɣletinyigbawo kple dzesiwo ƒe xexlẽdzesi bɔbɔe aɖe ko. Enye dziƒo ƒe akɔntabubugbe si woŋlɔ le ɣeyiɣi si tututu me wodzi wò. Védic Ɣletiviŋutinunya (jyotish), si dzɔ tso blema India, na xexeame ƒe ɣletiviŋutinunya ƒe gɔmeɖeɖe siwo de pɛpɛpɛ wu eye wòde to wu le gbɔgbɔ me le xexeame ƒe anyigbatata sia ŋu la dometɔ ɖeka.

---

## Nukae nye Védic Ɣletivimefakaka ƒe Nɔnɔmetata?

Védic Ɣletivimefakaka ƒe Nɔnɔmetata, si woyɔna tso blema ke be a**kundali ƒe nya**alo**dzidzi ƒe nɔnɔmetata**, nye anyigbatata si sɔ pɛpɛpɛ si dzi woŋlɔ ɣletinyigbawo ƒe nɔƒewo ɖo le wò dzidziɣi tututu. To vovo na Ɣetoɖoƒetɔwo ƒe ɣletivimefakaka la, jyotish zãa...**ɣletivimefakaka si woyɔna be sidereal**—afisi ɣletinyigbawo kple ɣletivihatsotsowo le ŋutɔŋutɔ le yame egbea—si wɔe be wòde pɛpɛpɛ ŋutɔ.

## Sidereal vs. Tropical Zodiac ƒe Nyateƒetoto

Ɣetoɖoƒetɔwo ƒe Ɣletivimefakaka zãa...**ɣletivimefakaka si le teƒe xɔdzowo**, si woɖo ɖi na ƒea ƒe ɣeyiɣiwo. Védic Ɣletivimefakaka zãa...**ɣletivimefakaka si woyɔna be sidereal**, si wotsɔ seke bla ɖe ɣletivimefakaka ƒe nɔƒe ŋutɔŋutɔ siwo ɣletiviwo le ŋu.

Le ɣeyiɣi siwo sɔ kple wo nɔewo ƒe do ŋgɔ ta la, vovototo si ade dzidzenu 23 le ɖoɖo eveawo dome fifia. Esia tae zi geɖe la, wò Védic Ɣleti ƒe Dzesi alo Dziƒodzesi toa vovo tso wò Ɣetoɖoƒetɔwo ƒe dzesi gbɔ—ewɔa ɖeka kple zã me yame ƒe nu ŋutɔŋutɔ tẽ.

## Wò Ɣeyiɣi ƒe Tsitrenɔnɔ Siwo Ŋu Ŋusẽ Le Gɔmesese

Védic ƒe nɔnɔmetata ƒe akpa siwo ŋu asixɔxɔ le wu dometɔ ɖekae nye ŋutete si le esi be wòawɔ ŋusẽ ƒe tsatsam ƒe nɔnɔmetata le ɣeyiɣi aɖe megbe. Ne míezã vimshottari dasha kple ɣletinyigbawo dzi mɔzɔzɔwo la, míate ŋu ase ɣletinyigbawo ƒe ɣeyiɣi siwo le ŋusẽ kpɔm ɖe wò tamesusuwo, lãmesẽ, kple ƒomedodo ƒe akpawo dzi fifia gɔme, si naa ahuhɔ̃e dzeani aɖe na ɖokuiwò nyanya kple susu ƒe agbenɔnɔ.
