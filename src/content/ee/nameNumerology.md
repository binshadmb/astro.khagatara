# Ŋkɔ Xexlẽdzesiwo: Ke Ðe Ŋusẽ si Le Wò Ŋkɔ Ŋu Ŋu

Menye le vo mee wotia wò ŋkɔ o. Le blema xexlẽdzesiwo ŋuti nunya nu la, ŋɔŋlɔdzesi ɖesiaɖe si le wò ŋkɔ me la tsɔa ʋuʋudedi ƒe ɣeyiɣi aɖe koŋ si kpɔa ŋusẽ ɖe wò amenyenye, ƒomedodowo, kple agbenɔnɔmɔ dzi. Kaldeatɔwo ƒe ɖoɖo si wozãna le Védic Xexlẽdzesiwo Ŋuti Nunya me la nɔa te ɖe eƒe gbeɖiɖi ƒe ʋuʋu dzi naa xexlẽdzesiwo ŋɔŋlɔdzesi ɖesiaɖe—ke menye eƒe nɔƒe le ŋɔŋlɔdzesiwo me ko o.

---

## Kaldeatɔwo ƒe Xexlẽdzesiwo Ŋuti Nuɖoanyi

Kaldeatɔwo ƒe nuɖoanyia nye xexeame ƒe blema xexlẽdzesiwo ƒe mɔnuwo dometɔ ɖeka, eye wòdzɔ tso Mesopotamia ƒe 4,000 kple edzivɔe nye sia. Togbɔ be ɖeko Pythagoras ƒe ɖoɖoa naa xexlẽdzesiwo tso 1 va ɖo 9 ɖe wo nɔewo yome hã la, Kaldeatɔwo ƒe ɖoɖoa naa asixɔxɔwo le gbeɖiɖi ƒe tsɔtsɔme nu, eye woɖea xexlẽdzesi 9 ɖa elabena wobunɛ be ele kɔkɔe:

*   **1:**A, Nye, J, Q, Y
*   **2: 1 .**B, K, R, kple R
*   **3: 1 .**C, G, L, S, kple 1999
*   **4: 1 .**D, M, T., kple 2019
*   **5: 1 .**E, H, N, X, kple X
*   **6: 1 .**U, V, W, kple W
*   **7: 1 .**O, Z, kple 1999
*   **8: 1 .**F, P., kple 100

## Ŋkɔ Xexlẽdzesi Vevi Etɔ̃awo

Xexlẽdzesi vevi etɔ̃e le wò ŋkɔwo ƒe nɔnɔmetata me:

1. 1. .**Nyagbɔgblɔ Xexlẽdzesi (Ŋkɔ Xexlẽdzesi):**Wobu akɔnta to wò dzigbeŋkɔ bliboa ƒe ŋɔŋlɔdzesiwo katã zazã me. Eɖea wò dzɔdzɔmeŋutetewo, ŋusẽ siwo le asiwò, kple alesi nèɖea wò agbemetameɖoɖo fiana xexeame fiana.
2. 2. .**Luʋɔ ƒe Ʋuʋu Xexlẽdzesi (Dzi ƒe Didi):**Wobu akɔnta to wò ŋkɔ ƒe ablɔɖegbeɖiɖiwo ɖeɖeko zazã me. Eɖea wò ememe ʋãme goglotɔwo kekeake, didi vevie le adzame, kple nusi naa wò luʋɔ dzea ŋuwò ŋutɔŋutɔ tso ememe la fiana.
3. 3. .**Amenyenye ƒe Xexlẽdzesi:**Wobu akɔnta to wò ŋkɔ ƒe xaxagbeɖiɖiwo ɖeɖeko zazã me. Eɖea alesi ame bubuwo bua wòe, alesi wòwɔa dɔ ɖe ame dzi enumake, kple gotagome amenyenye si nèɖena fiana la fiana.

## Afɔɖeɖe ɖesiaɖe ƒe Akɔntabubu ƒe Kpɔɖeŋu

Mina míabu akɔnta le xexlẽdzesiawo ŋu na ŋkɔa**MARIA**:
* .**Lɛtawo:**M (4) + A (1) + R (2) + I (1) + A (1) .
* .**Nyagbɔgblɔ ƒe Xexlẽdzesi:**4 + 1 + 2 + 1 + 1 = 9 *(Amegbetɔ ƒe Dɔwɔla)*
* .**Ablɔɖegbeɖiɖiwo (Luʋɔ ƒe Ʋuʋu):**A (1) + Nye (1) + A (1) = 3 *(Nuwɔwɔ)*
* .**Xaxagbeɖiɖiwo (Amenyenye):**M (4) + R (2) = 6 *(Vidzikpɔla)* .

## Ŋkɔwo ƒe Tɔtrɔ Kple Dekɔnu karma

Ame geɖe bua ŋkɔ tɔtrɔ alo megbeŋkɔ xɔxɔ le woƒe xexlẽdzesiwo sɔsrɔ̃ vɔ megbe ŋu. Ŋɔŋlɔdzesi ɖeka gɔ̃ hã tsɔtsɔ kpe ɖe eŋu alo eɖeɖeɖa trɔa ŋkɔa ƒe xexlẽdzesiwo ƒe ʋuʋu keŋkeŋ. Gake le Védictɔwo ƒe kɔnyinyi nu la, ŋkɔ si tututu wotsɔ na wò le dzidzi me lae lé wò ʋuʋudedi ƒe nɔnɔmetata kple karma si de to wu la ɖe asi. Wokafui vevie be nàsrɔ̃ nu tso wò dzigbeŋkɔ ŋu ahade bubu eŋu gbã hafi nàwɔ asitɔtrɔ le nyanya me.

## Un Espejo de tu Alma: Xexeame ƒe Nɔnɔmetata si Wowɔ na Ame ŋutɔ

Esia menye nyatakaka si wowɔ le vome si kɔmpiutadziɖoɖo siwo wozãna le mɔ vovovowo nu wɔ o. Enye xexeame ƒe anyigbatata si me kɔ, si tso dzime, si wobu akɔnta le akɔntabubu me pɛpɛpɛ to ɣletiviŋutinunya ƒe dzinu ƒe mɔzɔzɔ ŋutɔŋutɔwo kple tɔgbui Védic kple Kaldeatɔwo ƒe mɔfiamewo zazã me. Míaƒe taɖodzinu ɖeka koe nye be míana eme nakɔ, bubu, kple dɔwɔnu vavãtɔ si ana nàke ɖe ɖokuiwò ŋu ahatsɔ susu aɖo nu ŋu.

### Nukae le lalam le wò ŋutɔ wò axa 8 ƒe nɔnɔmetata (PDF) me?

*   **Srɔ̃ɖeɖe ƒe Ðekawɔwɔ & Ƒomedodo ƒe Ðekawɔwɔ:**Miaƒe ƒomedodo ƒe ɖoɖowo gɔmesese, alesi wò vidzidzi ƒe nɔnɔmetata hea ŋusẽ tɔxɛwoe, kple akɔntabubu ƒe safui siwo wozãna tsã be woatu srɔ̃ɖeɖe ƒe kadodo deto siwo nɔa anyi ɖaa si wotu ɖe ɖekawɔwɔ dzi ɖo.

*   **De dzesii si ku ɖe ŋugbledede ŋu:**Nyatakaka sia nye akɔntabubu ƒe ɖoɖowo ŋuti numekuku, si wowɔ be wòado alɔ ɖokuiŋububu kple ame ŋutɔ ƒe tsitsi.
