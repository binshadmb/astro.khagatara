## What is the Nakshatra or Birth Star?

The Nakshatra is your birth star—the lunar mansion where the Moon resided at your birth. Védic Astrology divides the zodiac into 27 Nakshatras, each governed by a specific energy, ruling deity, and purpose. Your Nakshatra reveals your deepest character, spiritual gifts, and the specific strengths you came to master in this life.

## The Dasha System: Planetary Time Cycles

The Dasha system is one of Védic Astrology's most profound tools. It divides your life into major periods ruled by different planets, each bringing a unique energetic influence. Understanding your current Dasha allows you to understand the prevailing planetary energy influencing your consciousness right now, supporting personal awareness and mindfulness.
